# Monthly report

**Timesheet** > **Monthly report** sums up the month for each person: hours, paid time, overtime, holidays, absences, night, Sunday and holiday work, and the days with a meal allowance and a commute refund.

![The monthly report of an approved month](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/monthly-report/report.webp)

## Use it

- Choose the month and **Everyone** or one person.
- **Day by day** shows every day of the month.
- **Download CSV** and **Download PDF** give you the report for your records or your accountant.
- Supervisors can approve or reopen a month from here.

## Timesheet rules

**Timesheet** > **Settings** holds the rules the report and payroll use: the correction period, paid breaks, daily hours, when night work starts and ends, the supplements for overtime, night, Sunday, holiday work and seniority, sick days paid by the employer, the meal allowance and the default rate for ticket invoicing.

![The timesheet rules](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/monthly-report/settings.webp)

Take the supplements from the collective agreement that applies to you. Slovenian holidays are added automatically, and you can add your own days off.
