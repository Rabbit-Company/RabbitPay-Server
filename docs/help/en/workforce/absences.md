# Absences

Vacation, sick leave and other absences are requested, approved and counted on the **Absences** tab of **Timesheet**.

## Request an absence

1. An employee presses **Request absence**. A supervisor presses **Add absence** and chooses the person.
2. Choose the **Type**, such as vacation or sick leave, and the dates **From** and **To**.
3. For part of a day, enter the hours per day. Leave it empty for whole days.
4. Press **Save changes**.

![Adding vacation at the end of the year](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/absences/new.webp)

## Approve or reject

A supervisor sees requests under **Pending** and presses **Approve** or **Reject**, with a note for the employee if needed. An absence a supervisor adds is approved right away.

![The absences of a person](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/absences/list.webp)

Approved absences count in the monthly report and in payroll, and the timesheet skips those days when it fills working days.

## Vacation balance

Under the week on the **Time** tab, the vacation box shows the days for the year, those carried over, taken, approved ahead and still waiting for approval, and the days left.

![The vacation balance](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/absences/balance.webp)

A supervisor can change **Days this year** and **Carried over** there.
