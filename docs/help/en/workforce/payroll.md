# Payroll

Calculate gross and net pay for the month from the timesheets, absences and employee records, with payslips, the REK-O file for eDavki and a payment file for the bank.

Press **Payroll** in the project menu.

![Payroll in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/nav.webp)

## 1. Prepare the tax table

Tax and contribution rates are kept as **Tax tables** that apply from a given month, so a change in the law is a new table and past runs stay as they were.

The first time, open **Tax tables** and press one of the **Start from** buttons, for example the current Slovenian table.

![No tax table yet](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/tax-tables.webp)

Compare every value with the official FURS and ZZZS tables. Then switch on **I checked every value against the official tables** and press **Save changes**. Payroll can only be finalized with a checked table.

![Confirming the tax table is checked](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/tax-table.webp)

![The checked tax table](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/tax-tables-saved.webp)

## 2. Calculate the month

1. On **Payroll runs**, press **New payroll run**.
2. Choose the **Month** and the **Payment date** and press **Calculate**.

   ![A new payroll run for September](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/new-run.webp)

RabbitPay works out gross pay, contributions, income tax and net pay for everyone with an employee record, and the employer's cost.

![The calculated payroll run](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/run.webp)

## 3. Bonuses and deductions

Press **Bonuses and deductions** next to a person to add a bonus, tax-free refunds or a deduction, then press **Recalculate**.

![Bonuses and deductions of one person](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/items.webp)

As long as the run is a draft, it follows the current timesheets every time you recalculate.

## 4. Finalize

Press **Finalize** and confirm when everything is right.

![Finalizing the run](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/finalize.webp)

From a finalized run you get:

- a **Payslip** PDF for each employee, or **All payslips** at once,
- **REK-O for eDavki**, the file to import and sign in eDavki,
- a **Bank payment file** with one salary payment per employee to upload in your online bank,
- **Export CSV** for your accountant.

![The finalized payroll run](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/payroll/finalized.webp)

**Reopen** brings the run back to a draft if something must change.

Severance pay, non-residents, student work, contractors and REK-O corrections are not handled yet. Check the results with your accountant before you pay.
