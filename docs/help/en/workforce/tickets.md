# Tickets

**Tickets** are tasks, bug reports, feature requests and support requests. Assign them, link them to a customer, log time on them and bill it.

## See your tickets

The list shows the number, title, status, priority, customer, who works on it and the time logged. Filter by status, person and text, and sort by activity.

![The list of tickets](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/tickets/list.webp)

**Board** shows the same tickets in columns by status. Change the status of a ticket with the menu on its card.

![The tickets as a board](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/tickets/board.webp)

## Add a ticket

Press **New ticket** and fill in the title, a description, the type, priority, status, customer and due date. Choose who works on it, and the hourly rate or a fixed price. Switch on **The customer can see this ticket in their portal** to share it with the customer, see [Tickets in the portal](customer-portal/tickets).

![A new ticket](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/tickets/new.webp)

## Work on a ticket

A ticket shows its description, attachments and comments, and on the right its details, the time logged, the time not invoiced yet and **Time by person**. Time is logged in the [timesheet](workforce/timesheet) by linking an entry to the ticket.

![A ticket with logged time](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/tickets/ticket.webp)

## Bill the time

1. Give the ticket an hourly rate, or a fixed price that is billed once whatever the time logged.
2. Press **Invoice ticket**. You can select several tickets of the same customer to combine them on one invoice.
3. Check the draft invoice that is created and issue it.

## Attach files

Open a ticket and press **Attach files**, or paste a screenshot anywhere on the page. A file can be up to 25 MB unless an owner or administrator changes that under **Files**, **Storage and limits**. Pictures up to 10 MB show a preview. Clicking the name of a video or a PDF opens it in the page, everything else is downloaded.

PNG and BMP pictures are stored as lossless WebP when that makes them smaller. Not a single pixel changes, only the file name ends in .webp. Photos and videos are stored as they are.

The person who uploaded a file, supervisors, administrators and owners can remove it. The ticket keeps a note of who removed it and when. Customers do not see attached files.

Attached files use **File storage**, which is separate from document storage, so a full file storage never stops you from issuing invoices. 10 GB comes with the workforce license and a file storage key adds more. Owners and administrators see every file, largest first, under **License**, **Manage files**, and can remove files there to free space.
