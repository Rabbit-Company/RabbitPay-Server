# Employee records

**Employees** holds what timesheets and payroll need to know about each person.

![Employees in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/employees/nav.webp)

## The list

Everyone who logs time is listed, with the employee number, job title, employment type, weekly hours, start date and pay.

![The employee records](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/employees/records.webp)

Press **Add details** for someone without a record, or **Edit** to change one.

## What a record holds

- **Job**: employee number, job title, employment type, start date, months of earlier service, weekly hours, vacation days and whether pay is monthly or hourly.
- **Pay**: the salary, which payroll uses as the base.
- **Personal details** payroll needs: tax number, address, bank account (IBAN), dependents, the general tax relief, commuting distance and whether this is a second job.
- Own timesheet rules when this person differs from the project rules.

![An employee record](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/employees/record.webp)

Records are stored encrypted, and only people with permission to see employee records can read them. Deleting a record keeps the timesheets and absences.

## Gross pay estimate

Under the list, **Gross pay estimate** works out pay for a month from logged hours, holidays and approved absences with the supplements from the timesheet rules. It is not a payslip: taxes and contributions are calculated in [payroll](workforce/payroll).

![The gross pay estimate](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/employees/estimate.webp)
