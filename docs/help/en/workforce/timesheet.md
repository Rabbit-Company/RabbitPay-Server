# Timesheets

Log working hours by day. A supervisor approves each person's month.

Press **Timesheet** in the project menu.

![Timesheet in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/timesheet/nav.webp)

## The week

The **Time** tab shows one week. Supervisors pick the person at the top, everyone else sees their own time. Move between weeks with the arrows or **This week**.

![A week of logged time](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/timesheet/week.webp)

Each day lists its entries with the start, the end, the type and the work category, and the paid time. Under the week you see the paid time and the expected time for that week.

## Add time

Press **+ Add time** on a day, or **Edit day** on a day that has time already.

1. Enter the **Start** and the **End**. An end earlier than the start continues into the next day.
2. Choose the type: regular hours, overtime or a break.
3. Mark remote work and choose the work category if you use them.
4. Add a note or link the time to a [ticket](workforce/tickets).
5. Changes after the correction period need a **Reason for the change**.
6. Press **Save changes**.

![Adding time for a day](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/timesheet/add-time.webp)

## Fill working days

**Fill working days** adds normal hours with a lunch break to every working day in a period that has no time yet. Slovenian public holidays, weekends and days with an absence are skipped.

![Filling the working days of a month](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/timesheet/fill.webp)

## Submit and approve the month

At the end of the month, the employee presses **Submit month**. The month is then locked for them.

A supervisor opens the person's timesheet and presses **Approve**, or **Return** with a reason so the employee can correct it.

![A submitted month waiting for approval](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/timesheet/approve.webp)

An approved month can be opened again with **Reopen**.

## Change history

Every change to time and absences is kept in the **Change history**, with who made it, when and why. The employee can read it too.

![The change history](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/workforce/timesheet/history.webp)
