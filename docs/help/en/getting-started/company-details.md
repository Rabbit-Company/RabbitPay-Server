# Fill in your company details

An invoice is only valid with the right details on it: who issued it, from where and with which tax number. Fill them in once and RabbitPay prints them on every document.

## Open Settings

Open your project and press **Settings** at the bottom of the project menu.

![Settings at the bottom of the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/company-details/open-settings.webp)

Settings is one long page of cards. Each card has its own save button, so save a card before you move on to the next one.

## Choose the language of your invoices

Do this first. RabbitPay uses this language for the wording it suggests in the next steps.

1. Find the card **Invoice**.
2. Under **Language**, choose the language your invoices and the payment page are written in.
3. Under **Date format** and **Time format**, choose how dates and times are written on them.
4. Check the **Accounting timezone**. It decides on which day an invoice is dated and numbered.
5. Press **Save invoice settings**.

   ![The Invoice card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/company-details/invoice-language.webp)

The sentence under the fields shows how an invoice issued today will read.

This is separate from the language of the interface, which every person picks for themselves in the [account menu](getting-started/find-your-way#change-the-language-and-the-theme). You can work in one language and invoice in another.

## Enter your company details

1. Find the card **Company details**.
2. Enter the **Legal name**, exactly as it is registered.
3. Enter the **Address**, **Postal code**, **City** and **Country**.
4. Enter your **Tax number** and your **Registration number**. If you are registered for VAT, enter your **VAT number** too, with the country letters in front, for example `IE3948217KH`.
5. Add the **Email**, **Phone** and **Website** you want printed on your invoices.
6. Use **Invoice footer** for anything else the law wants on your documents, such as a register entry. It is printed at the bottom of every invoice.
7. Press **Save company details**.

   ![The Company details card, filled in](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/company-details/company-details.webp)

> **Tip:** Is your company registered in Slovenia? Start typing its name or tax number under **Legal name** and pick it from the list. RabbitPay fills in the address, tax number and registration number from the public register of the Slovenian tax office (FURS). Companies elsewhere in the EU are found the same way by their VAT number.

If something required is still missing for your country, RabbitPay says so in orange above the save button, and where it can it offers the wording with **Add suggested text**.

## Set how VAT applies to you

1. Find the card **Tax**.
2. Under **Country you are established in**, choose the country where your business is registered for tax.
3. Under **VAT status**, choose the one that describes you. The table below explains them.
4. Check the **Reporting currency**. VAT amounts are reported to your tax office in this currency.
5. Press **Save tax settings**.

   ![The Tax card of a VAT registered company](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/company-details/tax.webp)

| VAT status                          | Choose it when                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **VAT registered**                  | You charge VAT and file VAT returns.                        |
| **Small business, exempt from VAT** | You are below the national threshold and do not charge VAT. |
| **Not registered for VAT**          | You are not a taxable business, or you are outside the EU.  |

From now on RabbitPay suggests the right VAT rate on every invoice line, including reverse charge for businesses in other EU countries.

Two fields appear only when they apply:

- **Registered for the EU One-Stop Shop (OSS)** is for VAT registered sellers in the EU who sell to consumers in other EU countries and declare that VAT in one return.
- **Exemption note** is for a small business. It is the sentence printed on every invoice that explains why no VAT is charged. Leave it empty to use the suggested wording.

## What comes next

Your details are in place. Now [tell customers where to pay and issue your first invoice](getting-started/first-invoice).

A Slovenian business has a few more things to set up before issuing invoices paid in cash or by card, such as fiscal verification with FURS. See [Slovenian compliance](slovenia).
