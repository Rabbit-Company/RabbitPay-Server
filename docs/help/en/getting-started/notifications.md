# Choose your notifications

RabbitPay can tell you when a customer pays, when an invoice becomes overdue, when a colleague writes to you and more. You decide what reaches you and how.

## Open your notification settings

1. Press your email address in the top right corner.
2. Choose **Notifications**.

   ![Notifications in the account menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/notifications/open-notifications.webp)

## The two ways RabbitPay reaches you

![In the browser and By email](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/notifications/channels.webp)

- **In the browser** shows a message in the corner of RabbitPay while you have it open.
- **By email** sends the same news to the address you sign in with.

### Get notifications when RabbitPay is closed

Switch on **Desktop notifications on this device**. Your browser asks for permission the first time. After that your browser shows a notification even when RabbitPay is in the background or closed.

- You decide this separately on every device.
- **Send a test** shows you what a notification looks like.
- The page lists every device that receives your notifications, so you can remove one you no longer use.
- A device stops receiving them when you sign out there, or after 30 days without you using RabbitPay on it.
- On an iPhone or iPad, add RabbitPay to the home screen first.

## Switch single notifications on or off

Below the two cards, everything RabbitPay can tell you about is grouped by area. Each line has one switch for **In browser** and one for **Email**.

![One group of notifications with its switches](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/notifications/choices.webp)

Your choices are saved as you make them and follow your account to every device. You only see the groups for the parts of RabbitPay you have access to.

![The whole Notifications page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/notifications/notifications.webp)

## What RabbitPay starts with

| Notification                                                | In browser | Email    |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------- |
| New chat messages, incoming calls, missed calls             | On         | Not sent |
| Meeting invitations and changes                             | On         | On       |
| Reminders 10 minutes before a meeting or event              | On         | Off      |
| Tickets assigned to you, tickets and replies from customers | On         | On       |
| Comments from colleagues on your tickets                    | On         | Off      |
| Absence requests and decisions                              | On         | On       |
| Timesheets waiting for your review                          | On         | Off      |
| Decisions on your timesheet                                 | On         | On       |
| New store orders                                            | On         | On       |
| Online payments, overdue invoices                           | On         | Off      |
| Fiscal verification problems                                | On         | Always   |

**Reset to defaults** brings these settings back.

## Good to know

- Emails about fiscal verification problems go to project owners and cannot be switched off, because a legal deadline depends on them.
- Emails about new store orders, online payments and overdue invoices count toward the project's email allowance, like emails to customers. All other notification emails are free.
- While your chat status is **Do not disturb**, nothing is shown in the browser. Emails are still sent.
