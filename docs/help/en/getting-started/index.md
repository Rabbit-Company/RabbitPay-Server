# Getting started

From a new account to your first paid invoice. Follow the pages below in order and you can send an invoice today. Each page is one small task with a picture of every step.

## What you will set up

| Step | What you do                                                          | Time      |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| 1    | [Create your account](getting-started/create-account)                | 1 minute  |
| 2    | [Protect it with a second step](getting-started/secure-your-account) | 3 minutes |
| 3    | [Create a project for your business](getting-started/create-project) | 1 minute  |
| 4    | [Learn where everything is](getting-started/find-your-way)           | 2 minutes |
| 5    | [Fill in your company details](getting-started/company-details)      | 3 minutes |
| 6    | [Issue and send your first invoice](getting-started/first-invoice)   | 5 minutes |
| 7    | [Invite your team](getting-started/invite-team)                      | 2 minutes |
| 8    | [Choose your notifications](getting-started/notifications)           | 1 minute  |
| 9    | [See what is included](getting-started/licenses)                     | 1 minute  |

## What you need

- An email address you can open.
- Your company's legal name, address and tax number.
- Your bank account number (IBAN), so customers know where to pay.

> **Tip:** Every page of this help has its own address. Hover over any heading and press **#** to copy a link that opens exactly that section, ready to send to a colleague.
