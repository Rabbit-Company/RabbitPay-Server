# Find your way around

Two minutes on this page save you a lot of searching later. RabbitPay has three places to look: the bar at the top, the project menu on the left and the menu under your email address.

## The top bar

![The bar at the top of every page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/find-your-way/top-bar.webp)

- **RabbitPay** and **Projects** take you back to the list of your projects.
- **Converter** converts amounts between currencies with the current exchange rates.
- Your email address on the right opens the [account menu](#the-account-menu).

## The project menu

When a project is open, its menu is on the left. It is split into four groups.

![The project menu on the left](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/find-your-way/project-menu.webp)

| Group       | What you find there                                                                                                           |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sales**   | **Invoices**, **Recurring** invoices, **Payments**, **Emails** you sent, **Customers**, **Items**, **Store** and **Terminal** |
| **Finance** | **Expenses** and **Statistics**                                                                                               |
| **Work**    | **Files**                                                                                                                     |
| **Project** | **Team**, **License** and **Settings**                                                                                        |

More entries appear when a module is active in the project: **Inventory** under Sales, **Accounting** under Finance, and **Timesheet**, **Tickets**, **Chat**, **Calendar**, **Employees** and **Payroll** under Work. See [what is included](getting-started/licenses).

You only see the entries your role allows. A cashier, for example, sees only the terminal.

## Go back to your projects

Press **All projects** above the project name, or **Projects** in the top bar.

![The link back to all projects](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/find-your-way/all-projects.webp)

The pill on the right shows your role in this project, for example **Owner**.

## The account menu

Press your email address in the top right corner.

![The account menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/find-your-way/account-menu.webp)

- **Account security** is where you change your email address and set up [two-factor authentication](getting-started/secure-your-account).
- **Notifications** is where you [choose what RabbitPay tells you about](getting-started/notifications).
- **Customer portal** opens the page where your customers see and pay their invoices.
- **Help** opens this help in a new tab.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** lists the shortcuts.
- **Sign out** ends your session on this device.

## Change the language and the theme

Both are in the account menu.

![Theme and Language in the account menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/find-your-way/theme-and-language.webp)

- **Theme** has three buttons: follow your device, always light, always dark.
- **Language** switches the interface between English and Slovenian.

These two settings are yours alone and are remembered on this device. The language of your invoices is a separate setting of the project. See [Choose the language of your invoices](getting-started/company-details#choose-the-language-of-your-invoices).

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **F1** anywhere to see the list.

![The list of keyboard shortcuts](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/find-your-way/keyboard-shortcuts.webp)

| Key                 | What it does                                            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `F2`                | Create a new invoice                                    |
| `F3`                | Create a new customer                                   |
| `F4`                | Create a new item                                       |
| `F6`                | Create a new expense                                    |
| `F7`                | Create a recurring invoice                              |
| `Ctrl` + `←` or `→` | Open the previous or the next entry of the project menu |
| `Ctrl` + `↑` or `↓` | Jump to the previous or the next group of the menu      |
| `Ctrl` + `Enter`    | Save or submit the form you are in                      |
| `Escape`            | Close the open window or menu                           |

The shortcuts that create something work while a project is open.

## On a phone

RabbitPay works in the browser of your phone or tablet, with nothing to install. The project menu folds into one button that shows the page you are on. Press it to see all the entries.

![A project on a phone](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/find-your-way/phone.webp)
