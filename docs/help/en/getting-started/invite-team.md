# Invite your team

Everyone who works with you gets their own account and a role that decides what they can see and do. Nobody shares a password.

## Send an invitation

1. In the project menu, press **Team**.
2. Press **Invite teammate**.

   ![The Team page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/invite-team/team.webp)

3. Enter the person's **Email**.
4. Choose their **Role**. The sentence under the list says what the role can do.
5. Press **Send invite**.

   ![Inviting an accountant](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/invite-team/invite.webp)

RabbitPay emails the person a link and also shows it to you, so you can send it yourself through another channel with **Copy invite link**.

![The invitation link](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/invite-team/invitation-link.webp)

> **Important:** Anyone who has the link can join with that role. Send it only to the person it is meant for.

## What the invited person does

1. They open the link from the email.
2. They create an account, or sign in if they already have one.
3. They accept the invitation. The project appears on their **Projects** page.

Until then the person is listed as **Pending**.

![The team list with a pending invitation](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/invite-team/invited.webp)

For a pending invitation you can press **Copy link** to copy the link again, **Email again** to send the email once more, or **Remove** to withdraw it.

## Choose the right role

| Role              | What they can do                                                             |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**         | Everything, including deleting the project and managing the team             |
| **Administrator** | Everything except deleting the project                                       |
| **Manager**       | Payments, invoices, customers and recurring invoices                         |
| **Accountant**    | See the financial data and export it, without changing it                    |
| **Developer**     | Manage API keys and webhooks for connecting other software                   |
| **Viewer**        | Look, but not change anything                                                |
| **Cashier**       | Sell at the terminal and see their own sales, nothing else                   |
| **Supervisor**    | Manage everyone's timesheets, absences and tickets and run work hour reports |
| **Employee**      | Log their own working hours and absences and work on tickets                 |

Give each person the smallest role that lets them do their work. The roles **Supervisor** and **Employee** are for the [timesheets and tickets](workforce) module.

## Change a role or remove someone

1. Open **Team**.
2. To change what a person can do, choose another role in the **Role** column next to their name.
3. To take away their access, press **Remove** in their row.

You cannot change or remove your own membership. Another owner or administrator has to do that.
