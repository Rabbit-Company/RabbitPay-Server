# Create your account

Your account is how you sign in to RabbitPay. One account can run several businesses, and creating it takes about a minute.

## Register

1. Open [rabbitpay.net](https://rabbitpay.net) and press **Get started** in the top right corner.

   ![The home page, with Get started in the top right corner](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/home.webp)

2. Enter your **Email** and choose a **Password**. Use a long password that you do not use anywhere else. Your password never leaves your browser as you typed it.
3. Tick the box to accept the Terms of Service. Both documents open in a new tab if you want to read them first.
4. Press **Create account**.

   ![The registration form, filled in](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/register.webp)

You are signed in right away and land on **Projects**, which is still empty.

![The Projects page of a new account](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/signed-in.webp)

The next step is to [protect your account](getting-started/secure-your-account), then to [create your first project](getting-started/create-project).

## Sign in

1. Open [rabbitpay.net/login](https://rabbitpay.net/login), or press **Sign in** on the home page.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Press **Sign in**.

   ![The sign-in form](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/sign-in.webp)

If you switched on two-factor authentication, RabbitPay asks for your security key or a code next. See [Sign in with your second step](getting-started/secure-your-account#sign-in-with-your-second-step).

> **Note:** The link **Customer portal** under the form is for people who received an invoice from a business that uses RabbitPay. As the business, you always use the form above.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in form, press **Forgot your password?**.

   ![Forgot your password? under the Sign in button](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/forgot-password.webp)

2. Enter the **Email** you sign in with and press **Send the link**.

   ![Asking for a link to choose a new password](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/request-link.webp)

   RabbitPay confirms that the link is on its way.

   ![The confirmation that a link was sent](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/link-sent.webp)

3. Open the email from RabbitPay and press **Choose a new password**. The link works once and expires after one hour.
4. Enter the **New password** twice and press **Save the new password**.

   ![Choosing a new password](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/choose-password.webp)

5. Sign in with the new password.

Every device that was signed in is signed out. Two-factor authentication stays as it was, so you still need your second step to sign in. For safety, RabbitPay shows the same message whether or not an account uses the address you entered.

To change a password you still know, see [Change your password](getting-started/secure-your-account#change-your-password).

## Sign out

1. Press your email address in the top right corner.
2. Press **Sign out**.

   ![The account menu, with Sign out at the bottom](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/create-account/sign-out.webp)

Always sign out on a computer that other people use.

## Change your email address

1. Press your email address in the top right corner and choose **Account security**.
2. Under **Email address**, enter the **New email address** and your **Password**.
3. Press **Change email**. RabbitPay sends a confirmation link to the new address.
4. Open the link within one hour. From then on you sign in with the new address.

Your projects, invoices and settings stay with your account.
