# Protect your account

Two-factor authentication adds a second step after your password, so a stolen password alone does not open your invoices and your customers' data. Setting it up takes three minutes.

## Open Account security

1. Press your email address in the top right corner.
2. Choose **Account security**.

   ![The account menu, with Account security at the top](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/open-account-security.webp)

## Set up an authenticator app

An authenticator app shows a new 6-digit code every 30 seconds. Install one on your phone first, for example Aegis, 2FAS or Google Authenticator. They are free.

1. Under **Authenticator app**, press **Set up authenticator app**.

   ![The Authenticator app card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/set-up-authenticator.webp)

2. Open the app on your phone, add a new account and scan the QR code on the screen. If you cannot scan it, press **Copy secret** and type or paste the secret into the app.

   ![The QR code and the secret for setting up by hand](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/scan-code.webp)

3. Enter your RabbitPay **Password** and the 6-digit **Authenticator code** the app shows now.
4. Press **Connect authenticator app**.

   ![The password and the code, ready to confirm](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/confirm-code.webp)

The setup expires after 10 minutes. If it does, press **Set up authenticator app** again and scan the new code.

## Save your recovery codes

Right after you connect the app, RabbitPay shows ten recovery codes. Each one works once in place of a code from your phone.

![The recovery codes, shown only once](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/recovery-codes.webp)

1. Press **Copy all recovery codes**.
2. Paste them into your password manager, or print them and keep the paper somewhere safe.

> **Important:** The codes are shown only this one time. If you lose your phone and have no recovery codes, you cannot sign in. Save them before you leave the page.

The card at the top now says **Enabled**.

![Two-factor authentication is switched on](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/enabled.webp)

## Sign in with your second step

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and press **Sign in**, as always.
2. Enter the code from your app under **Authenticator or recovery code**.
3. Press **Sign in** again.

   ![The sign-in form asking for the code](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/sign-in-code.webp)

No phone at hand? Enter one of your recovery codes in the same field. That code is then used up.

## Add a security key

A security key is the strongest second step. It can be a hardware key such as a YubiKey, or a passkey kept by your phone, computer or password manager. You can add several.

1. On **Account security**, find **Security keys**.
2. Give the key a **Name** that tells you which one it is, and enter your **Password**.
3. Press **Add security key** and follow what your browser asks, for example touching the key or using your fingerprint.

   ![Naming a new security key](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/add-security-key.webp)

The key appears in the list, with the date it was added and when it was last used.

![The security key in the list](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/security-key-added.webp)

From now on RabbitPay asks for the key right after your password. Press **Use security key** if the request does not appear by itself. You can still type a code from your app instead.

## Replace your recovery codes

Do this when you have used several codes, or when you think someone else has seen them.

1. On **Account security**, find **Replace recovery codes**.
2. Enter a current **Authenticator code**, or leave the field empty to confirm with your security key.
3. Press **Generate new recovery codes** and save the new ones. The old codes stop working at once.

## Remove a second step

- **Remove** next to a security key removes that key.
- **Remove authenticator app** disconnects the app. Codes from it stop working.
- **Disable two-factor authentication** removes every key, the app and the recovery codes, so you sign in with only your password again.

![The whole Account security page with two-factor authentication on](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/account-security.webp)

## Change your password

1. On **Account security**, find **Password**.
2. Enter your **Current password**, then the **New password** twice.
3. If two-factor authentication is on, enter a code from your app, or leave the field empty to confirm with your security key.
4. Press **Change password**.

   ![The Password card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/getting-started/secure-your-account/change-password.webp)

You stay signed in on this device. Every other device is signed out, and RabbitPay emails you that the password was changed. If you no longer know your password, see [Forgot your password](getting-started/create-account#forgot-your-password).

## Download your data

Under **Your data**, **Download my data** saves a copy of the personal data kept about your account: your profile, security settings, accepted terms, project memberships and activity logs. Invoices and other business records are exported from each project instead.
