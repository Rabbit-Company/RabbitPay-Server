# Import an e-invoice

When a supplier sends you an e-invoice, you do not have to type it in. RabbitPay reads the file and fills in the expense for you. e-SLOG 2.0 and Peppol UBL 2.1 invoices are supported.

## Import the file

1. On **Expenses**, press **Import e-invoice**.

   ![Import e-invoice at the top of the Expenses page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/import-e-invoice/import-button.webp)

2. Choose the XML file the supplier sent you.

RabbitPay opens the expense form already filled in: the supplier, the invoice number, the dates, the amounts, the VAT, the supplier's IBAN and the payment reference.

![An expense filled in from an e-invoice](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/import-e-invoice/review.webp)

## Check and save it

1. Read the note at the top. It names the file and the format.
2. Choose the **Category**. The e-invoice does not say what the cost is for in your business.
3. Check the VAT.
4. Press **Add expense**.

   ![The checked expense, ready to save](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/import-e-invoice/save.webp)

The original XML is attached to the expense, because it is the legal original of the invoice.

![The imported expense in the list](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/import-e-invoice/imported.webp)

## Warnings to look out for

RabbitPay warns you above the form when:

- the invoice is addressed to another company,
- you have to account for the VAT yourself, for example on reverse charge,
- the invoice is in a foreign currency,
- the totals in the file do not add up,
- an expense with the same supplier and invoice number already exists. Saving would record it twice.

Read the warning before you save. Nothing is saved until you press the button.
