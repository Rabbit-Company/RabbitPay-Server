# Income and expenses

See what you invoiced, what you spent and what is left, for any period, by month or by year.

## Open Statistics

In the project menu, press **Statistics**. **Income and expenses** is at the top.

![Statistics in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/financial-report/open-statistics.webp)

## Generate the report

1. Set the **From** and **To** dates.
2. Under **Period**, choose **Monthly** for one row per month or **Yearly** for one row per year.
3. Press **Generate report**.

   ![The report before it is generated](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/financial-report/empty.webp)

The report is calculated once and saved. Everyone in the project sees the same saved result, with the date and time it was made. After generating, wait a few minutes before you generate it again. Changing the dates only affects the next report you generate.

## Read the report

![Income and expenses for this year](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/financial-report/report.webp)

| Figure                       | What it contains                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Net revenue**              | Issued invoices without tax, reduced by credit notes               |
| **Expense costs**            | Expenses without the VAT you can reclaim                           |
| **Payment fees**             | What payment providers kept                                        |
| **Estimated profit or loss** | Net revenue less expense costs and payment fees                    |
| **Cash flow**                | Payments received less refunds, payment fees and expenses you paid |

**Expenses by category** below the table shows where the money went.

Each currency is shown separately.

## Download it

**Download CSV** saves the report that is on screen, for example for your accountant. Amounts in the file are in cents.

> **Note:** These figures are estimates from the records in RabbitPay. They leave out accounting adjustments such as depreciation, so use them to follow the business and leave the annual accounts to your accountant.
