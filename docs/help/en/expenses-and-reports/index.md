# Expenses and reports

Record what your business spends, bring in supplier invoices without retyping them, pay suppliers in one batch and see income, expenses and VAT side by side.

## Record what you spend

| I want to                                  | Page                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enter a supplier invoice or a receipt      | [Record an expense](expenses-and-reports/record-expense)      |
| Bring in a supplier's e-invoice            | [Import an e-invoice](expenses-and-reports/import-e-invoice)  |
| Bring in many expenses from a spreadsheet  | [Import from CSV](expenses-and-reports/import-csv)            |
| Enter rent and subscriptions automatically | [Recurring expenses](expenses-and-reports/recurring-expenses) |
| Pay several suppliers at once from my bank | [Pay suppliers](expenses-and-reports/pay-suppliers)           |

## See how the business is doing

| I want to                            | Page                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| See income, costs and profit         | [Income and expenses](expenses-and-reports/financial-report) |
| See the VAT I owe on sales           | [VAT report](expenses-and-reports/vat-report)                |
| See which products and services sell | [Item sales](expenses-and-reports/item-sales)                |

## Who can do what

Owners, administrators, managers and accountants can record and edit expenses. Only owners and administrators can delete them. Everyone who can see reports can open **Statistics**.
