# Record an expense

An expense is anything your business pays for: a supplier invoice, a receipt, rent or a subscription. Record it once and it appears in your reports and in what you still owe.

## Open Expenses

In the project menu, press **Expenses**.

![Expenses in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/open-expenses.webp)

The page lists your expenses, newest first, with **Recurring expenses** below them. The buttons at the top start everything on this page.

![The Expenses page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/expenses.webp)

## Add an expense

1. Press **Add expense**, or press `F6` anywhere in the project.
2. Enter a **Description**, for example `Postage and courier, October`.
3. Choose the **Supplier**. Start typing a name and pick it from the list, or type a new name.
4. Choose a **Category**. Pick one of the common ones or type your own.

   ![Choosing a category](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/choose-category.webp)

5. Enter the **Total amount** the supplier charged, including VAT.
6. Check **VAT/tax shown on invoice**. RabbitPay estimates it from the total at your standard rate. Change it when the invoice has a reduced rate, several rates or no VAT, or press **Use 23% estimate** to go back to the estimate.
7. Under **Reclaimable VAT/tax**, choose how much of that VAT your business can reclaim: **All**, **None** or a **Custom amount**. A VAT registered business usually reclaims all of it.

   ![The amount, the VAT and what can be reclaimed](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/amounts.webp)

The line below the amounts shows the real cost to your business, the total less the VAT you reclaim.

## Keep the original invoice with it

1. Enter the **Supplier invoice number**.
2. Under **Original supplier invoice**, choose the PDF, a photo of the receipt or the e-invoice XML. Up to 20 MB.

The file is kept with the expense, so you have the original when your accountant or the tax office asks for it.

## Dates and payment

1. **Expense date** is the day the cost belongs to. Your reports use this date.
2. **Due date** is when the supplier expects to be paid. Leave it empty for something you already paid.
3. If you will pay by bank transfer, enter **Pay to IBAN** and the **Payment reference**. Without an IBAN, the supplier's account is used. See [Pay suppliers](expenses-and-reports/pay-suppliers).
4. Switch on **Paid** if the expense is already paid, set the **Payment date** and choose **Paid with**: **Bank transfer**, **Card** or **Cash**. Card and cash receipts get their own list in the [Accountant package](expenses-and-reports/accountant-package).
5. Press **Add expense**.

   ![A finished expense, ready to add](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/filled.webp)

The expense appears at the top of the list.

![The new expense in the list](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/list.webp)

## Change, mark as paid or delete an expense

The **Paid** switch in each row marks an expense as paid or unpaid in one click. **Edit** opens the expense to change it. **Delete** removes it from your reports.

![The switch and the buttons in an expense row](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/row-actions.webp)

## Find an expense

Use **Status** to show only paid or unpaid expenses, and **From** and **To** to show one period.

![Only the expenses that are still unpaid](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/record-expense/unpaid.webp)

## Categories without reclaimable VAT

The VAT on some costs usually cannot be reclaimed. When you choose **Representation**, such as client lunches, **Fines and penalties** or **Donations**, RabbitPay sets **Reclaimable VAT/tax** to **None** for you. Change it if your tax adviser tells you otherwise.
