# Item sales

See which of your saved [items](invoices/items) sell, how much they bring in and how much is still waiting for payment.

## Generate the report

1. Open **Statistics** and scroll to **Item sales**.
2. Choose the period, for example **Last 30 days** or **This year**.
3. Press **Generate report**.

## Read the report

![Item sales for the last 30 days](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/expenses-and-reports/item-sales/report.webp)

The two numbers at the top show what was sold in each currency, with the amount still awaiting payment, and how many different items were sold.

| Column               | What it shows                                                        |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Item**             | The item and its SKU                                                 |
| **Sold**             | The quantity on paid invoices                                        |
| **Revenue**          | The line totals before invoice discounts and tax                     |
| **Share**            | How it compares to your best seller                                  |
| **Invoices**         | On how many invoices it was                                          |
| **Awaiting payment** | The quantity and amount on invoices that are issued but not paid yet |

Only lines that were picked from **Items** count. A line typed by hand is not in this report.
