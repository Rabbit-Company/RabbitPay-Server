# Required company details

The Companies Act (ZGD-1, Article 45) says what a Slovenian company prints on the documents it sends:

- its full name and registered office,
- the register it is entered in and its registration number,
- for a d.o.o. or a d.d., its share capital and any part not yet paid in.

The name, the address and the registration number come from **Settings** > **Company details** and are printed on every invoice. The register entry and the share capital belong in the **Invoice footer**.

## Let RabbitPay suggest the footer

1. Open **Settings** and find **Company details**.
2. Under the footer, RabbitPay says what is still missing. Press **Add suggested text**.

   ![RabbitPay points out the missing register entry and share capital](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/slovenia/company-details/missing.webp)

3. The usual sentences appear in the footer, with `___` where your details go.

   ![The suggested sentences with blanks](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/slovenia/company-details/suggested.webp)

4. Replace every `___`, for example with the court that keeps your entry and the amount of your share capital.
5. Press **Save company details**.

   ![The finished footer](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/slovenia/company-details/filled.webp)

The footer is printed at the bottom of every invoice, credit note and pro forma invoice.

> **Tip:** The sentences are written in the language of your invoices. Change the language under **Settings** > **Invoice** first if you need them in Slovenian.

## Sole traders

A sole trader (s.p.) prints the full name and address and does not need a share capital sentence. RabbitPay asks only for the registration number.
