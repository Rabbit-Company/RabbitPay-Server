# Domestic reverse charge

For some supplies between two Slovenian VAT payers, the buyer accounts for the VAT instead of you, under Article 76.a of ZDDV-1, and the invoice carries none.

The most common case is construction work, including fitting out and installation.

You must be registered for VAT and the customer must have a Slovenian VAT ID.

## Set it on the item

1. Open **Items** and press **New item**, or edit an existing one.
2. Set **VAT category** to **Domestic reverse charge**. Keep the normal rate in **Tax %**.

   ![An installation service with the domestic reverse charge](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/slovenia/reverse-charge/item.webp)

3. Save the item.

The rate stays on the item because other customers, for example people or businesses that are not VAT payers, are still charged the normal rate.

## Invoice it

Check the customer's VAT number first. Open the customer, press **Edit** and then **Save and check in VIES**. Without a confirmed VAT number, the line keeps the normal rate and the invoice form tells you why.

Then add the item to an invoice for that customer. The line is suggested at 0% and marked **Domestic reverse charge**.

![The line at 0% under the domestic reverse charge](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/slovenia/reverse-charge/invoice.webp)

The issued invoice prints the note the law requires.

## Without an item

On a line with 0% VAT, choose the reason under **Why no VAT is charged**, for example the domestic reverse charge.

## Good to know

- Reverse charge supplies and supplies with VAT go on separate invoices.
- A domestic line with VAT can only use the rates 22%, 9.5% or 5%.
- **Statistics** has a list of these sales by customer, which you need for the PD-O form.
