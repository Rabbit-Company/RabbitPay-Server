# Slovenian compliance

What a Slovenian business sets up in RabbitPay: fiscal verification with FURS, e-invoices, the details the law wants on every invoice and the VAT records you submit in eDavki.

Everything here applies to projects whose tax country is Slovenia. You choose it, together with your VAT status, under **Settings** > **Tax**, as described in [Company details](getting-started/company-details). A Slovenian project always reports VAT in euros.

## Set up once

| I want to                                         | Page                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Take cash, cards or crypto with FURS verification | [Fiscal verification](slovenia/fiscal-verification)             |
| Print the register entry and share capital        | [Required company details](slovenia/company-details)            |
| Sign my e-invoices                                | [e-Invoices (e-SLOG)](slovenia/e-invoices#sign-your-e-invoices) |

## Day to day

| I want to                                           | Page                                                     |

| Send an e-invoice to a company or the public sector | [e-Invoices (e-SLOG)](slovenia/e-invoices)               |
| Invoice construction work without VAT               | [Domestic reverse charge](slovenia/reverse-charge)       |
| Invoice in dollars or for last month                | [VAT periods and other currencies](slovenia/vat-periods) |

Selling in a shop or at a market? The [Terminal](terminal) works with fiscal verification too.

Only the ordinary VAT rules are covered. Cash accounting, the margin scheme and self-billing are not supported.
