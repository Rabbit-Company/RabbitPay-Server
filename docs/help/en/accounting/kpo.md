# KPO for sole traders

The book of revenue and expenses for sole traders who keep simplified books, prepared from the same postings as the journal.

## Turn it on

The **KPO** tab appears when **Bookkeeping** under [Chart of accounts](accounting/start#choose-the-kind-of-books) is set to **Sole trader, simplified bookkeeping**.

## Read the book

Open **Accounting** > **KPO** and choose the year. The cards at the top show revenue, expenses and the result. Below, every posting has a sequence number, date, description and its amount in the right column.

![The KPO for a year](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/kpo/book.webp)

The columns are sales revenue, other revenue, material and goods, services, labour, depreciation, interest, taxes and contributions and other expenses. Only columns with amounts are shown.

Invoices, expenses, payroll and depreciation appear in the book on their own. **Download CSV** saves it for your records or your accountant.
