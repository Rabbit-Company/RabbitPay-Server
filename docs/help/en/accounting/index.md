# Accounting

Keep the books of a Slovenian company or sole trader in the same project you invoice from. Documents post to the ledger on their own.

RabbitPay keeps a double entry ledger by the Slovenian accounting standards (SRS). Invoices, payments, expenses, payroll and stock post on their own, so you mostly check, match and close. Account names come from the official Slovenian chart of accounts, so they stay in Slovenian even when RabbitPay is in English.
