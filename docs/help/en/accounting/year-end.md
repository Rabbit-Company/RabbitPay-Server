# Year end

Revalue foreign currency items, adjust the deductible VAT share and close the year, so its result is posted and the balances carry forward.

## The business years

Open **Accounting** > **Year end**. Every year with postings is listed with its number of entries, its result so far and whether it is closed.

![Last year ready to close](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/year-end/years.webp)

A year can be closed after 31 December, once the earlier years are closed. Do the steps below first, in this order.

## Revalue foreign currency items

Receivables and payables in another currency that are still open on 31 December are converted at that day's reference rate.

Press **Revalue foreign currency**. RabbitPay lists the open items in each currency. Enter the euro value of one unit from the ECB or Banka Slovenije for 31 December and press **Post revaluation**.

![The revaluation dialog](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/year-end/revalue.webp)

The difference posts to financial revenue or expenses on 31 December and is reversed on 1 January. To revalue again, reverse that entry in the journal first.

## Adjust the deductible VAT share

If part of your VAT is not deductible because some of your sales are exempt, you deduct during the year with a provisional share. Mark such expenses with **VAT deducted with the provisional deductible share** in the expense form.

At year end press **Deductible share**, enter the final share and save.

![The final deductible share](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/year-end/share.webp)

The difference between the VAT you deducted and the VAT at the final share is posted on 31 December against the costs or, for fixed assets, their value. RabbitPay posts this in the books and the evidence. Report the same correction on your DDV-O return yourself.

## Close the year

Press **Close year** and confirm.

![Closing the year](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/year-end/close.webp)

RabbitPay then:

1. Moves revenue and expenses to the result of the year, a profit to **9320** or a loss to **9330**. For a sole trader it goes to **9350** or **9370**.
2. Posts a closing sheet that brings every balance sheet account to zero on 31 December.
3. Posts an opening sheet that opens them again on 1 January of the next year.
4. Locks the year. Invoices, expenses and entries dated in it cannot be changed.

![The closed year](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/year-end/closed.webp)

Closing is refused if some documents could not be posted. Fix them first, as listed above the journal.

## Reopen a year

Found a mistake after closing? Press **Reopen**, write the reason and confirm. The result, closing and opening sheets are reversed and the year is unlocked. The reason is kept in the audit log.

![Reopening a year with a reason](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/year-end/reopen.webp)

Reopen the following year first if it is closed too. Correct the documents, then close the year again.
