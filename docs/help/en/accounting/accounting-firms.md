# Accounting firms

Keep the books of many companies from one list, with what needs attention at each of them.

## Your clients

Every company whose books you keep is its own project, with its own books and its own accounting license. A company that uses RabbitPay [invites you](getting-started/invite-team) with the **Accountant** role. For a company that does not use RabbitPay, you add the project yourself.

Press **All clients** on any accounting tab to open the list.

![All clients on the accounting tabs](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/accounting-firms/all-clients.webp)

## The client list

The list shows every company with its accounting license, the entries this year, the last posting and the last month whose DDV evidence was submitted.

![The list of accounting clients](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/accounting-firms/clients.webp)

**Needs attention** lists what is waiting at each company:

- documents that could not be posted
- years not closed
- bank lines not matched yet
- expenses without the supplier's invoice attached
- unpaid expenses

Click a company to open its journal.

## Add a client

Press **Add client**, enter the business name and press **Add client**. You become the owner of the new project.

![Adding a client](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/accounting-firms/add-client.webp)

Fill in the company details in its [Settings](getting-started/company-details) next. Then redeem an accounting license for it and record the opening balances in the [journal](accounting/journal).

When a client's accounting license is not active, the notice on every accounting tab lets you redeem a new key yourself, without the owner.
