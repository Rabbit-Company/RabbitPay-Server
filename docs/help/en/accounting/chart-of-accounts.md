# Chart of accounts

The accounts the books use, and where each expense category posts. Add accounts of your own inside the official classes.

## The accounts

Open **Accounting** > **Chart of accounts**. The chart is based on the Slovenian chart of accounts (Enotni kontni okvir 2024) and the recommended chart that goes with it, with a four digit code for every account.

![The first accounts of the chart](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/chart-of-accounts/accounts.webp)

- **Edit** renames an account.
- **Deactivate** hides an account you do not use from the account pickers. Accounts that documents post to cannot be deactivated.

Every bank account gets an account of its own. The first IBAN uses **1100**, further ones take the next free code.

## Add an account

Press **New account**, enter a code of 2 to 8 digits and a name. The first digit decides the class, for example 4 for costs or 7 for revenue and expenses.

![A new account for warehouse rent](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/chart-of-accounts/new-account.webp)

## Where expenses post

Each expense category posts to one account. Change it under **Expense categories**. All expenses in that category are posted again to the new account.

![Expense categories and their accounts](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/chart-of-accounts/categories.webp)

Expenses marked as fixed assets do not use this table. They post to the asset account of their group in the [fixed asset register](accounting/fixed-assets).
