# Open items and IOP

Unpaid invoices and supplier bills by partner, with their age, and a printed statement of open items (IOP) to confirm balances.

## See what is open

Open **Accounting** > **Open items**. Choose the date under **As of** and whether to show customers, suppliers or both.

![Open items with one partner opened](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/open-items/list.webp)

The cards at the top add up open receivables, overdue receivables and open payables. Each partner row splits the balance by age, from **Not due** to **Over 180 days**, counted from the due date. Expenses without a due date are counted from their date.

- **Items** shows the invoices, credit notes and payments behind the balance.
- **Search partner** finds a partner by name or tax number.
- **Export CSV** downloads the list.

Partners with the same tax number are shown together, even if their name is written differently on some documents. Manual entries on customer and supplier accounts count too, when they have a partner.

## Print an IOP

A statement of open items (izpisek odprtih postavk) asks the partner to confirm the balance, which auditors and year end checks often need.

Press **IOP** on a partner row, or **Print IOP for all** for every partner with a balance. Each statement lists the open items on the chosen date, with a place for the partner to confirm or dispute the balance and sign.

![An IOP ready to print](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/open-items/iop.webp)

Press **Print**, or save it as a PDF from the print dialog.

## A difference on an account

If an account has an amount that is not linked to any document or partner, for example from a manual entry without a partner, a notice above the list names the account and the amount. Add the partner with a correcting entry in the [journal](accounting/journal).
