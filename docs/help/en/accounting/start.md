# Start with accounting

Turn on accounting with a license, choose the kind of books the business keeps and see what RabbitPay posts for you.

## Open Accounting

Accounting is in the menu under **Finance** once the project has an accounting license. Redeem the key on the [License](getting-started/licenses#redeem-a-license-key) page.

![Accounting in the menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/start/nav.webp)

The tabs along the top lead to every part of the books: **Journal**, **Issued elsewhere**, **Bank**, **Open items**, **Fixed assets**, **Trial balance**, **Statements**, **AJPES**, **Year end** and **Chart of accounts**.

## Choose the kind of books

Open **Chart of accounts** and choose under **Bookkeeping** how the business keeps its books.

![The bookkeeping choice](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/start/bookkeeping.webp)

| Choice                                  | For                                  | What changes                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Company, double entry**               | A d.o.o., d.d. or other company      | All tabs. The result of the year closes to the company's equity.                                                            |
| **Sole trader, double entry**           | A s.p. that keeps double entry books | All tabs. The result closes to the sole trader's income and moves into the owner's capital in the next year.                |
| **Sole trader, simplified bookkeeping** | A s.p. that keeps simplified books   | Adds the [KPO](accounting/kpo), the book of revenue and expenses.                                                           |
| **Sole trader, flat rate expenses**     | A s.p. taxed with flat rate expenses | Only the tabs a flat rate business needs: journal, invoices issued elsewhere, bank, fixed assets and the chart of accounts. |

## What posts on its own

You do not type in invoices or expenses twice. Each of these posts to the journal with the right accounts as soon as it exists:

- issued invoices, credit notes, advance invoices, payments and refunds
- expenses and their payments
- [invoices issued elsewhere](accounting/issued-elsewhere) and [bank statement lines](accounting/bank-statements) you match or book
- monthly depreciation of [fixed assets](accounting/fixed-assets)
- finalized [payroll](workforce/payroll) runs
- stock receipts, issues and the cost of goods sold, if you use [Inventory](inventory)

When a document changes, the old entry is reversed and a new one is posted. Nothing in the journal is ever edited or deleted, so the history stays complete.

## When the license ends

Documents keep posting and the books stay readable, so you can always print them. Manual entries, matching bank lines and changes to accounts wait until a license is active again. The notice on every tab lets you enter a new key straight away.

![The notice shown without an active license](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/start/license.webp)

## Who can use it

Owners, administrators and members with the **Accountant** role can post entries and change the books. Other roles that can see reports can read them. To bring in your accountant, [invite them](getting-started/invite-team) with the Accountant role.
