# Financial statements and AJPES

The balance sheet and income statement prepared from the ledger, and the annual report for AJPES as an XML file to import.

## Balance sheet and income statement

Open **Accounting** > **Statements** and choose the **Business year**. The statements follow SRS 20 for small and micro companies and version I of the income statement in SRS 21, with the previous year beside it.

![The balance sheet and income statement](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/financial-statements/statements.webp)

**Download CSV** saves them for a spreadsheet. Close the year first, so the result is final.

RabbitPay warns you when something does not add up:

- assets and equity and liabilities differ
- the income statement does not match the result in the ledger
- an account has a balance but no place in the statements, usually an account added outside the chart

## The AJPES annual report

Open **AJPES**. The same statements are shown with the AOP codes of the AJPES form, for companies or for sole traders depending on your [bookkeeping choice](accounting/start#choose-the-kind-of-books).

![The AJPES report with AOP codes](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/financial-statements/ajpes.webp)

1. Press **Download XML for AJPES**.
2. In the AJPES application for annual reports, choose **Uvoz XML** and import the file.
3. Add the details AJPES asks for, such as the size of the company, check the report and submit it.

Turn on **Show lines without amounts** to see every AOP line of the form. You can also copy the amounts by hand.

> **Tip:** Have your accountant check the statements before you submit them. RabbitPay prepares them from the ledger, so they are only as complete as the books.
