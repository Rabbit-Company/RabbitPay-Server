# Trial balance and account cards

The opening balance, turnover and closing balance of every account for a period, and every posting on one account.

## Read the trial balance

Open **Accounting** > **Trial balance** and choose the period. The period always stays inside one business year, and revenue and expense accounts start the year at zero.

![The trial balance for last year](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/trial-balance/accounts.webp)

Each account shows the opening debit and credit, the debit and credit turnover in the period and the balance at the end. Every class of the chart ends with a subtotal, and the last row adds up the whole ledger. Debit and credit totals are always equal.

## Show groups or classes

Change **Detail** to **Account groups** or **Account classes** for a shorter overview.

![The trial balance by class](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/trial-balance/classes.webp)

**Export CSV** downloads the trial balance with the detail you chose.

## Open an account card

Click an account in the trial balance, or in the journal, to see every posting on it with a running balance.

![The account card of the bank account](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/trial-balance/account-card.webp)

Type a name under **Partner** to see only the postings of one customer or supplier. **Export CSV** downloads the card.
