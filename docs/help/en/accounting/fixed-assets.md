# Fixed assets

Keep the register of equipment, computers and other fixed assets. RabbitPay depreciates them every month and writes them off when disposed.

## Register a bought asset

When you record an expense for equipment, set **Purchase type** to **Other fixed asset** in the expense form. It then waits under **Expenses to register** on **Accounting** > **Fixed assets**.

1. Choose the **Group**, for example **Computers and software**.
2. Press **Add to register**.

![A laptop waiting to be registered](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/fixed-assets/candidates.webp)

The annual rate is filled in with the highest rate the tax authority recognizes for the group.

## The register

The register shows every asset with its cost, annual rate, depreciation so far and book value.

![The register with the laptop](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/fixed-assets/register.webp)

Depreciation is straight line, starting on the first day of the month after the asset is ready for use. It posts at the end of each month on its own.

## Add an asset from before

For assets bought before you started with RabbitPay, press **New asset**. Enter the cost, the depreciation before and, if it differs, the date to depreciate from.

![The form for an older asset](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/accounting/fixed-assets/new.webp)

Post the same cost and depreciation as part of your opening entry in the [journal](accounting/journal), so the register and the ledger agree.

## Sell or scrap an asset

Press **Dispose** and enter the date. Depreciation stops at the start of that month and the remaining book value is written off.

**Delete** removes an asset entered by mistake and reverses its depreciation. The expense it came from stays.
