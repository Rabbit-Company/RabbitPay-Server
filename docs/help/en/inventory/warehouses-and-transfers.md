# Warehouses and transfers

Stock is kept and valued separately for each warehouse. Every project starts with one, the default warehouse that sales take from.

## Add a warehouse

1. Open the **Settings** tab and press **New warehouse**.
2. Enter the **Name**, a short **Code** and the **Address**.
3. Press **Save changes**.

![A second warehouse for the shop](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/warehouses-and-transfers/new-warehouse.webp)

![The warehouses of the project](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/warehouses-and-transfers/warehouses.webp)

**Make default** changes which warehouse sales take from.

## Move stock

1. Choose **More documents** > **New transfer**.
2. Choose the warehouse the goods leave and the one they go **To**.
3. Add the items and quantities and press **Post**.

![Moving bags and stickers to the shop](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/warehouses-and-transfers/transfer.webp)

The goods keep their purchase value. Invoices and the online store take from the default warehouse, so transfer goods there before selling them, or let the [terminal](inventory/sales#terminal-and-online-store) sell from the shop.
