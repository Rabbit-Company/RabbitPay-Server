# Start keeping stock

Choose which items keep stock and enter what is on your shelves today. From then on every sale reduces it.

## Open Inventory

Press **Inventory** in the project menu.

![Inventory in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/start/nav.webp)

At first no item keeps stock.

![Inventory before any item keeps stock](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/start/empty.webp)

## Keep stock for an item

1. Press **Keep stock for an item**.
2. Choose the **Item**. Only items of the type goods can keep stock. Services and license keys cannot.
3. Choose the **Stock type**. **Goods** are bought to be resold. **Material** is used up in your work or production.
4. Optionally enter a **Minimum stock**. The item is marked **Low** when it falls below it, and it is suggested for reordering.
5. Under **Stock you have today**, enter the **Quantity on hand** and the **Purchase price per unit** without VAT.
6. Choose the **Ledger entry**. Pick **None** when your accountant already has this stock in the opening balance. Pick **Recognise the stock and reduce costs** when the goods were booked as a cost when you bought them.
7. Press **Start keeping stock**.

![Keeping stock for a print, with 12 on hand](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/start/dialog.webp)

If the item is sold in the [online store](online-store), the dialog shows the stock number the store had and proposes it as the quantity on hand. From now on the store shows the stock kept here.

From that moment every issued invoice with this item reduces its stock. Invoices issued before that moment are not touched.

## Your stock

Repeat for every item. The **Stock** tab lists them with the quantity, the minimum, the unit cost and the value, and the totals at the top.

![The stock of six items](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/start/stock.webp)

You can also start from **Items**: the list shows the stock of every item and a **Keep stock** shortcut.

To enter the stock of many items at once, use **More documents** > **Opening stock**, which works like a receipt.
