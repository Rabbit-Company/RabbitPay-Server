# Suppliers and purchase orders

Purchase orders are optional. Use them when you want to know what is on its way and what a supplier still owes you.

## Add a supplier

1. Open the **Suppliers** tab and press **New supplier**.

   ![The Suppliers tab](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/suppliers-empty.webp)

2. Enter the **Name**. For a Slovenian company, type the name or tax number and pick it from the FURS register, and the address fills itself in.
3. Add the email, the payment terms in days and the **IBAN**. The IBAN is used in [payment files](expenses-and-reports/pay-suppliers).
4. Press **Save changes**.

   ![A new supplier](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/supplier.webp)

![The list of suppliers](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/suppliers.webp)

## Know what to reorder

The **Orders** tab lists under **Items to reorder** every item whose stock together with open orders is below its minimum, with a suggested quantity and the last supplier.

![An item below its minimum](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/reorder.webp)

Press **Create order** to open an order with the missing quantities and the last purchase prices, or **New purchase order** to start an empty one.

## Write the order

1. Choose the **Supplier** and check the **Order date**, the **Expected delivery** and where to **Deliver to**.
2. Enter their offer number under **Supplier offer or reference**.
3. Add the items, quantities and agreed prices without VAT.
4. Press **Confirm order**, or **Save draft** to finish it later.

   ![A purchase order for three mugs](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/order.webp)

5. Confirm. The order gets its number and can no longer be changed.

   ![Confirming the order](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/confirm.webp)

![The confirmed order](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/confirmed.webp)

Press **Print** for the order to send to the supplier.

![The printed purchase order](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/printed.webp)

## Receive against the order

When the goods arrive, open the order and press **Receive goods**. The receipt opens with the open quantities and prices, so you only correct what really arrived and add the delivery note number.

![The receipt prepared from the order](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/receive.webp)

A delivery in several parts is simply several receipts. The order closes itself when everything has arrived, and shows the ordered, received and invoiced values side by side, so a supplier invoice that does not match stands out.

![The order after the goods arrived](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/suppliers-and-orders/received.webp)

**Close order** ends an order earlier when nothing more is expected. Ordered quantities show as on order until then.
