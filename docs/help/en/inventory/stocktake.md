# Stocktake

The Companies Act (ZGD-1) requires you to compare the books with the real state at least once a year, usually on 31 December.

## Count

1. Choose **More documents** > **New stocktake**, then the date and the warehouse.
2. Press **Print count sheet** for a list to count from. **Count sheet without booked stock** leaves the expected numbers out, so the counters do not copy them.

   ![A count sheet for the stocktake committee](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/stocktake/count-sheet.webp)

3. Press **Add all items** and enter the **Counted** quantity for each. Under every line you see the booked quantity and the difference. Items you leave out are not changed. Enter 0 for an item that is not there at all.
4. Decide whether shortages are charged with VAT. A shortage above the normal loss is, see [Issue stock for other reasons](inventory/issue-stock#which-reason).
5. Press **Post** and confirm.

![Counting, with two cards missing](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/stocktake/count.webp)

Surpluses and shortages are booked at the current purchase value.

![The posted stocktake](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/stocktake/posted.webp)

## After the stocktake

Nothing can be booked for the counted items on or before its date. Press **Print** on the posted stocktake for the record the stocktake committee signs.

When a year with stock has no posted stocktake, the year list reminds you before you close it.
