# Issue stock for other reasons

Use **More documents** > **New issue** for everything that leaves stock without a sale.

## Enter an issue

1. Choose the **Reason**. The text under it explains what happens.
2. Add the items and quantities. The current stock is shown under each line.
3. Write what happened under **Notes**.
4. Press **Post** and confirm.

## Which reason

| Reason                                          | What it means                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Used in the business**                        | Material or goods used up at work. Becomes a material cost.                    |
| **Damage or breakage**, **Expired or obsolete** | Written off. Keep a record of the destruction.                                 |
| **Normal loss (kalo)**                          | Loss within the norm of your trade association. No VAT is due.                 |
| **Shortage above the normal loss**              | Counts as a supply, so VAT is due on the purchase value.                       |
| **Private or own use**                          | Taken for private use. VAT is usually due on the purchase value.               |
| **Gift or sample**                              | Given away. VAT is usually due unless it is a sample or a gift of small value. |
| **Theft**                                       | No VAT when the police made a report.                                          |
| **Force majeure**                               | Fire, flood or a similar event. No VAT is due.                                 |
| **Return to the supplier**                      | Goods sent back. Record the supplier's credit note as a stock purchase.        |

When VAT is due, leave **Charge VAT on the purchase value** switched on. The VAT is posted and listed in the DDV records as an internal document.

## Cancel a document

A posted document cannot be changed. Open it, press **Cancel document** and give the reason.

![Canceling a document](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/issue-stock/cancel.webp)

Its movements are removed, everything issued later is valued again and the ledger entry is reversed. The document stays in the list as canceled and keeps its number. Then enter a new, correct document. This works for every type of document.
