# Stock reports

See what you have now, every stock document and the stock as it stood on any day.

## Stock now

The **Stock** tab shows every item with its quantity, minimum, unit cost and value, and the totals of goods and material at the top. Filter by warehouse, search or show only items **Below minimum**, **Below zero** or **Out of stock**.

![The stock of all items](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/stock-reports/stock.webp)

## All documents

The **Documents** tab lists every receipt, issue, transfer, stocktake and opening stock, with drafts, posted and canceled ones. Filter by type and status, or search by number or supplier.

![The list of stock documents](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/stock-reports/documents.webp)

## Stock on a date

**Stock on a date** shows quantities and values as they stood at the end of any day, for example 31 December for the balance sheet. Choose the date and the warehouse, and press **Download CSV** or **Print**.

![Stock on a date](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/inventory/stock-reports/valuation.webp)

## Item card

The card of every item lists each receipt, sale and issue with the quantity and value after it. See [Sales and returns](inventory/sales#follow-an-item).
