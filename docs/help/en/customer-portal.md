# Customer portal

The customer portal is where the people and companies you invoice find their documents and pay them. It is separate from the dashboard you work in, and your customers need no password.

## How customers sign in

Customers open `/customer/login` on your RabbitPay address, or choose **Customer portal** on the home page.

1. They enter their email address and press **Send login link**.
2. They open the link in the email and confirm. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes.

They see the documents that were issued to that email address, so make sure the email on your customer record is the one your customer uses. The email is saved on the invoice when it is issued. Changing a customer's email later does not move earlier invoices to the new address.

The portal sends email, so email must be set up on the server.

## What customers can do

- See their invoices from every business on the same RabbitPay server, filtered by **Unpaid**, **Overdue** and **Paid**.
- Open an invoice, print it, download the PDF and see related credit notes.
- Pay an open invoice through the payment page.
- See and pay open pro forma invoices.
- Download the invoice as e-SLOG when the document carries their VAT ID or tax number.

Drafts are never shown.

## Orders and saved details

Customers of your online store see their orders under **Your orders**. Under **Profile** they can save default billing and delivery details, which are filled in at the next checkout.

## Support tickets

Customers do not see tickets unless you allow it. A team member who works with tickets gives a customer access to the portal and marks the tickets that customer may see. The customer can then read those tickets, reply and open new ones. See [Timesheets, tickets and payroll](workforce).

## Their data

Under **Profile** > **Privacy and your data**, a customer can download everything stored about them with **Download my data** and remove their portal account with **Delete my account**. Deleting the account removes the profile and the sign in right away. Issued invoices stay with you, because tax law requires you to keep them.
