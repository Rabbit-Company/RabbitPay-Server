# Coupons

Give customers a code for a discount at checkout.

## Add a coupon

1. Open **Store** > **Coupons** and press **New coupon**.
2. Enter a **Code** or press **Generate**. Customers can type it in any case.
3. Choose the **Type**: a percentage off, a fixed amount off or free shipping, and enter the value.
4. Optionally set a **Minimum order**, a period with **Valid from** and **Valid until**, how many **Total uses** it has and whether **Each customer can use it once**.
5. Press **New coupon**.

## Follow how they are used

The list shows how often each coupon was used and how much discount it gave. Switch off **Active** to stop a coupon without deleting it.

Discounts apply to the products, not to shipping, and are counted on prices with VAT. A canceled order gives its use back.
