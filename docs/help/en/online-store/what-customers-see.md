# What customers see

Press **Open store** on any **Store** tab to see the store the way customers do.

## The home page

![The home page of the store](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/what-customers-see/home.webp)

At the top is the announcement bar, then the menu with **Categories**, search, the customer account and the cart. Below the hero you find the advantages of shopping with you, your categories and your featured products.

The first time a customer visits, a short note explains that the store keeps only the cart and the sign in on their device. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

![The privacy note](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/what-customers-see/privacy-notice.webp)

## A category

Customers can filter by your specifications, show only what is in stock and sort the products.

![The Prints category with filters](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/what-customers-see/category.webp)

## A product

The product page shows the photos, the price with VAT, the stock, when the order should arrive and the description and specifications.

![A product page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/what-customers-see/product.webp)
