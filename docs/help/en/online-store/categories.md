# Categories

Categories group your products. They appear in the store menu, on the home page and as filters.

## Add a category

1. Open **Store** > **Categories** and press **New category**.

   ![The Categories tab before any category exists](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/categories/empty.webp)

2. Enter the **Name**. The **Web address** is filled in from the name, or you choose your own.
3. Optionally choose a **Parent category** to make it a subcategory, and add a **Description** that is shown at the top of the category page.
4. Press **Add category**.

   ![A new Prints category](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/categories/new.webp)

![The categories of the store](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/categories/list.webp)

**Order** decides the order in the menu, lower numbers first. A category page also shows the products of its subcategories.

Put products into a category on the product itself. See [Put products in the store](online-store/products).
