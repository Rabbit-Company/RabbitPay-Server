# Checkout and payment

What your customer goes through, from the cart to paying, and what you get.

## The cart

Customers press **Add to cart** on a product. A small window confirms it and offers the way to the cart or the checkout.

![A product was added to the cart](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/added.webp)

The cart shows the products, the total with VAT, the delivery estimate and the payment methods you accept.

![The cart](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/cart.webp)

## Sign in with email

There are no passwords. At checkout, the customer enters their email address and gets a sign in link.

![Signing in at checkout](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/sign-in.webp)

![The link was sent](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/link-sent.webp)

The link opens a short confirmation. After pressing the button, the customer is back at checkout, signed in. The cart stays on their device in the meantime.

![Confirming the sign in](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/confirm-sign-in.webp)

## The order form

1. **Your details**: name, address and phone, or a company with its VAT ID if you allow business customers.

   ![The customer's details](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/details.webp)

2. **Delivery**: another delivery address if needed, and the shipping method.

   ![Delivery and shipping method](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/delivery.webp)

3. The summary on the right shows the total. A coupon code is entered there.

   ![The summary with a coupon](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/summary.webp)

4. **Review and pay**: an optional note, and the customer accepts your terms of sale and privacy policy.

   ![Accepting the terms and placing the order](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/place-order.webp)

## After the order

The customer sees their order with its number and a button to pay right away. They also get an email with the order and the payment link.

![The order was placed](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/checkout/order-placed.webp)

The products are held for them until the payment deadline. An order that is still unpaid 7 days after its payment deadline is canceled automatically, and its stock is released.

## When the order becomes an invoice

An order is not an invoice yet. It has an order number such as `ORDER-26000001` and waits for payment.

The invoice is issued when the first payment arrives, by card, crypto or a bank transfer that you record. It gets the next invoice number and is sent to the customer as a PDF, followed by any license keys. Because only paid orders get an invoice number, abandoned orders leave no gaps and need no credit notes.

For a bank transfer, record the payment on the order's invoice when the money arrives. See [Record a payment](invoices/record-payment).

Customers find all their orders and invoices under the account icon in the store, where they can also export or delete their data.
