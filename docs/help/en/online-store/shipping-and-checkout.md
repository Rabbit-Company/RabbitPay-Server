# Shipping, pickup and checkout

Tell customers how they get their order and how long they have to pay. Everything here is on the **Settings** tab of **Store**.

## Shipping options

Under **Shipping and delivery**, every option has a name, a price, an optional amount above which it is free and the number of days it takes. Prices include VAT.

1. Press **+ Add a shipping option**.
2. Enter the **Name**, for example `An Post, standard post`, the **Price** and the delivery days.
3. Enter **Free from** to make it free for larger orders, or leave it empty.

![A post option and pickup at the studio](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/shipping-and-checkout/shipping.webp)

For pickup, add an option with the price 0 and switch on **In store pickup**. Pickup options are offered only when the physical store is switched on, see below.

Products that are only downloaded, such as license keys, never need shipping.

## Preparation time

**Days at least** and **Days at most** under **Preparation time** say how long you take to send an order. Together with the shipping days, the store shows customers when their order should arrive. Orders after the **Order cutoff hour** are prepared the next day, and with **Count only working days** weekends are skipped.

A product can have its own preparation time. See [Put products in the store](online-store/products).

## Physical store

If customers can visit you, switch on **We have a physical location** under **Physical store** and enter the address, a note and the opening hours.

![The physical store with its address](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/shipping-and-checkout/location.webp)

The store then shows the address and the opening hours in the footer, says whether you are open right now and offers your pickup options.

## Checkout

![The Checkout card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/shipping-and-checkout/checkout.webp)

- **Days to pay** is how long a customer has to pay an order. It becomes the due date of the invoice.
- **Allow business customers** lets customers order as a company, with the company name and VAT ID on the invoice.
- **Allow order notes** gives customers a field for a message with their order.

Press **Save** at the bottom of the page.
