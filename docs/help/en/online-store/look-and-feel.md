# Make it look like yours

Everything about how the store looks is in the **Appearance** card on the **Settings** tab of **Store**.

![The Appearance card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/look-and-feel/appearance.webp)

## Logo and hero image

- **Logo** is shown at the top of every page. A square image works best.
- **Hero image** is the large picture on the home page.

Press **Upload image** and choose a file. Images are made smaller and converted in your browser before they are uploaded.

## Colors and style

- **Accent color** is used for buttons, links and the announcement bar. Pick one of the swatches or your own color.
- **Color scheme**: **Follow the visitor's device** shows the store light or dark like the rest of their phone or computer. You can also always show it light or always dark.
- **Font**, **Corners**, **Product cards** and **Products per row** change the feel of the whole store.

## The home page

- **Hero style** decides how the top of the home page looks, for example **Text beside image**.
- **Hero title** and **Hero text** are the big headline and the sentence under it.
- **Button text** and **Button link** add a button, for example **Shop the prints** that opens the Prints category. Leave the link empty to link to all products.

Products with **Feature on the home page** switched on are shown on the home page. See [Put products in the store](online-store/products).

## Announcement bar

A short line at the very top of every page, for example a free shipping offer. Enter it in **Announcement bar** under **General**, or leave it empty to hide the bar.

![The announcement bar setting](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/look-and-feel/announcement.webp)

Press **Save** at the bottom of the page when you are done. Open the store to see the result.

## Custom CSS

For changes the settings do not cover, open **Custom CSS** and write your own styles. They are applied on top of the store theme, and the store's elements use classes that start with `sf-`.
