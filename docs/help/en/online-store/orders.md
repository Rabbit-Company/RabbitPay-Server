# Handle orders

Open **Store** > **Orders**. A number on the tab shows how many paid orders wait to be sent.

## Find the orders to send

Choose a view at the top:

- **Paid, to ship**: what to send next.
- **Waiting for payment**: orders not paid yet.
- **Paid** and **All orders**.

![A new order waiting for payment](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/orders/unpaid.webp)

Once paid, the order moves to **Paid, to ship**. Press **Open**.

![A paid order waiting to be sent](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/orders/to-ship.webp)

## The order

The order shows the products, the discount, the total, the customer and where it goes. **Open invoice** takes you to its invoice.

![An order with the customer and delivery address](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/orders/order.webp)

## Send it

1. Press **Start preparing** when you begin packing. The customer is told.
2. When it leaves, paste the **Tracking link** from the carrier.
3. Press **Mark as shipped**.

![The tracking link and Mark as shipped](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/orders/ship.webp)

4. Press **Mark as delivered** when it has arrived.

![The order has been shipped](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/orders/shipped.webp)

With **Email the customer about this change** switched on, every step sends the customer an email, with the tracking link when it is shipped. Customers can also follow the order in their account.

## Cancel an order

**Cancel order** on an order that has not shipped returns the stock and closes the order. If it was already paid, refund the payment on its invoice, as described in [Return money](invoices/record-payment#return-money).
