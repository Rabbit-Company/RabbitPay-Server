# Put products in the store

Every item under **Items** can be sold in the store. The store adds photos, a description and stock to it.

The price, the VAT and the SKU always come from the item, so they are the same on invoices, at the terminal and in the store.

## Find the item

Open **Store** > **Products**. It lists all your items, the ones in the store and the ones that are not. Press **Add to store** next to an item.

![All items, ready to be added to the store](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/products/list.webp)

To sell something new, first add it under **Items**. See [Items](invoices/items).

## Describe it

Under **Product page**:

- **Name in the store** replaces the item name in the store only. Invoices and receipts keep the item name.
- **Short summary** is one or two sentences shown on product cards.
- **Description** is the long text on the product page, written in Markdown. The preview next to it shows how it looks.

![The text of a product](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/products/content.webp)

If the store has more languages, each one has its own tab. See [Translations](online-store/translations).

## Visibility, price and stock

On the right:

- **For sale in the store** shows the product to customers. Switch it off to hide it without losing anything.
- **Feature on the home page** puts it on the home page.
- **Category** and **Web address**.
- **Was price** shows a crossed out older price next to the real one.
- **Track stock** with **Quantity in stock** stops selling the product when it runs out. **Keep selling when out of stock** takes preorders instead, and **Expected restock** tells customers when it is back.
- **Use a custom preparation time** if this product takes longer than the rest.

![Visibility, price and stock of a product](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/products/side.webp)

Press **Add to store**.

Items that sell license keys take their stock from the keys. Items kept in [inventory](inventory) take it from the warehouse.

## Photos

Once the product is in the store, press **+ Add photos**. Up to 12 photos per product. The first one is the main photo shown on cards. Move photos with the arrows and describe each one for people who use screen readers.

![Two photos of a print](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/products/images.webp)

## Specifications

Add specifications such as **Size** **A3**. They are shown on the product page as a table, and customers filter products by them on category pages.

![Specifications of a product](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/products/attributes.webp)

Press **Save** after every change.

## All products at a glance

Filter the list by **For sale**, by category or by search. The list shows the price customers pay, the stock and whether a product is featured.

![The products in the store](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/products/published.webp)
