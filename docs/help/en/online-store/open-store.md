# Open your store

Create the store, give it a name and a web address, and check the legal pages before customers arrive.

## Before you start

- Fill in your company details under **Settings** > **Company details**. They are printed on invoices and filled into the legal pages. See [Company details](getting-started/company-details).
- Switch on at least one [payment method](payments/payment-methods).
- The online store is a paid module. If your project does not have it yet, **Store** shows what it includes and a button to redeem a license. See [What is included](getting-started/licenses).

## Create the store

1. Press **Store** in the project menu.

   ![Store in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/open-store/nav.webp)

2. Until you create it, the store says it is not set up yet.

   ![The store before it is created](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/open-store/setup.webp)

3. Under **General**, enter the **Store name**, a **Tagline** and the **Web address**. The web address is the last part of the link, so `harbour-lane` gives `https://rabbitpay.net/shop/harbour-lane`.
4. Choose the **Default language**, the language shoppers see first.
5. Leave **Store is open** on if customers may order right away, or switch it off to prepare the store in peace.

   ![The General card of a new store](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/open-store/general.webp)

6. Press **Create store** at the bottom of the page.

The store is live at its address. **Open store** at the top opens it in a new tab.

![The new store and its address](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/open-store/created.webp)

## Check the legal pages

A new store starts with a **Privacy policy**, **Terms of sale** and a **Right of withdrawal** page, already filled in with your company details. You find them under **Pages and legal texts**.

![The legal pages of a new store](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/online-store/open-store/legal-pages.webp)

Open each one, read it and adjust it to how you sell. The privacy policy and the terms of sale are required, because customers accept them at checkout. You can add up to 12 pages of your own, for example **About us** or **Size guide**, written in Markdown.

## Close the store for a while

Switch off **Store is open** and save. The store shows a closed page and takes no orders until you switch it on again. Your products, orders and settings stay as they are.

## Use your own domain

Under **Custom domain**, enter a domain such as `shop.example.com` and press **Connect**. RabbitPay shows the DNS records to add at your domain provider. Press **Check now** once they are added. The store then opens on your domain, and emails and links use it too.
