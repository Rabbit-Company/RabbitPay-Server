# Print and download

Get an invoice on paper, as a PDF or as an e-invoice, one at a time or many at once.

## Open the Documents menu

Open the invoice and press **Documents**.

![The Documents menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/print-and-download/documents-menu.webp)

- **Print** opens the invoice on its own page, ready for the printer.
- **Download PDF** saves it as a PDF file.
- **Download e-SLOG** saves an issued invoice as an e-invoice in the Slovenian e-SLOG format. A draft has no e-invoice.

## Print an invoice

1. Choose **Documents** > **Print**.
2. Press **Print** in the top right corner and choose your printer.

   ![The invoice on its own page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/print-and-download/printed-invoice.webp)

**Back to invoice** takes you back.

> **Tip:** If a web address, a date or a page number appears at the edge of the paper, your browser added it. Untick **Headers and footers** in the print window to remove it.

With a bank transfer switched on, the invoice carries your account details and a QR code that a banking app can scan.

## Download many invoices at once

For example to hand a month of invoices to your accountant.

1. On **Invoices**, press **Download PDFs**.

   ![Download PDFs above the invoice list](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/print-and-download/download-button.webp)

2. Under **By date**, choose a **Period** such as **Previous month**, or set **Issued from** and **Issued to** yourself.

   ![Choosing the invoices by date](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/print-and-download/download-by-date.webp)

3. Or press **By invoice number** and choose the first and the last invoice. Both and every invoice between them are included.

   ![Choosing the invoices by number](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/print-and-download/download-by-number.webp)

4. Press the download button. RabbitPay says how many invoices it found before you download.

You get one ZIP file with a PDF for every invoice.

- One file holds up to 1000 invoices. For more, choose a shorter period.
- Drafts and pro forma invoices are left out.
- Canceled invoices are included, because their numbers were used.
