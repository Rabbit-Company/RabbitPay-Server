# Customers

Save a customer once and pick them on every invoice. Each customer has a page that shows what they owe and how they pay.

## Open Customers

In the project menu, press **Customers**.

![Customers in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/customers/open-customers.webp)

![The customer list](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/customers/customers.webp)

## Add a customer

1. Press **New customer**, or press `F3` anywhere in the project.
2. Enter the **Name**. For a company in Slovenia, start typing its name or tax number and pick it from the list. The address and the tax numbers are filled in from the public register of the tax office. For a company elsewhere in the EU, type its VAT number.
3. Enter the **Email** that invoices are sent to. Without it, invoices can only be printed or downloaded.
4. Under **Type**, choose **Business** or **Individual**. A business in another EU country can be invoiced with reverse charge.
5. Fill in the **Address**, **City**, **Postal code** and **Country**.
6. For a business, enter the **VAT number** if it is VAT registered, or the **Tax number** if it is not.
7. Press **Create customer**. With a VAT number filled in, the button **Save and check in VIES** also checks the number in the EU register.

   ![Adding a private person as a customer](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/customers/new-customer.webp)

You can also add a customer while writing an invoice, with **Add a new customer** in the **Customer** field.

**e-Invoice details** at the bottom of the form are for public sector buyers in Slovenia, who receive e-invoices through UJP. See [Slovenian compliance](slovenia).

## Find, change or delete a customer

Type a name, an email address or a VAT number into the search field above the list.

![Searching the customers](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/customers/search.webp)

Each row has three buttons.

![The buttons in a customer row](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/customers/row-actions.webp)

- **Invoices** opens the invoice list filtered to this customer.
- **Edit** changes the details. Invoices already issued keep the details they were issued with.
- **Delete** removes a customer who has no invoices.

The column **VAT** shows the VAT number and whether it was checked: **Valid**, **Invalid** or **Unchecked**.

## Read a customer's page

Press the name of a customer to open their page.

![The page of one customer](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/customers/profile.webp)

| Part                        | What it shows                                                                                              |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The four numbers at the top | Invoices issued, how many are unpaid and past due, how many were paid on time, and the average time to pay |
| **Totals**                  | What was billed, received and is outstanding, for each currency                                            |
| **Details**                 | The contact and tax details                                                                                |
| **Payment history**         | The first and the last invoice and the last payment                                                        |
| **Recurring invoices**      | What is billed to this customer on a schedule                                                              |
| **Invoices**                | Every invoice of this customer                                                                             |

The buttons at the top start the usual next steps.

![The buttons on a customer's page](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/customers/profile-actions.webp)

- **Edit** changes the details.
- **Open in invoices** shows the customer's invoices in the invoice list, where you can filter them further.
- **New invoice** starts an invoice with this customer already chosen.
