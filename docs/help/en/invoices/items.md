# Items

Items are the products and services you sell. Save each one once with its price, unit and tax rate, and pick it on every invoice instead of typing it again.

## Open Items

In the project menu, press **Items**.

![Items in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/items/open-items.webp)

![The item list](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/items/items.webp)

## Add an item

1. Press **New item**, or press `F4` anywhere in the project.
2. Enter the **Name**. It becomes the description of the invoice line.
3. Enter a **SKU** if you use codes of your own.
4. Enter the **Unit price** without VAT, and choose the **Unit**, for example hour, piece or month. The unit is printed on the invoice.
5. Under **What it is**, choose **Service**, **Digital service** or **Goods**. This decides how VAT applies when you sell across borders.
6. Under **VAT category**, choose **Standard rate**, **Reduced rate**, **Exempt from VAT** or **Domestic reverse charge**. The **Tax %** is filled in with the rate of your country, and you can change it.
7. Press **Add item**.

   ![A new item](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/items/new-item.webp)

**Notes** are only shown here, to tell similar items apart.

![The new item in the list](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/items/item-added.webp)

## Use an item on an invoice

On the invoice form, press the description of a line and start typing the name or the SKU. Pick the item, and the description, unit, price and tax rate are filled in. Then enter the quantity.

![Picking a saved item on an invoice line](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/items/pick-item.webp)

You can still change anything on the line for this one invoice. An item priced in another currency is converted at today's rate.

## Change, archive or delete an item

Each row has four buttons.

![The buttons in an item row](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/items/row-actions.webp)

- **Keys** manages the license keys of an item that sells them. See below.
- **Edit** changes the item. Invoices already issued keep the description and price they were issued with.
- **Archive** hides the item from new invoices and keeps it on record. **Show archived** above the list brings archived items back into view, where **Restore** makes one active again.
- **Delete** removes an item that was never used. An item that is on an invoice can only be archived.

## Sell license keys

For software or vouchers, an item can hand out one key per unit sold.

1. Switch on **Sells license keys** when you add or edit the item.
2. Press **Keys** in its row and paste your keys, one per line.

Each unit sold takes the oldest key that is left and emails it to the customer the moment the invoice is paid in full. A key is never given out twice. The list shows how many keys are in stock, held for unpaid invoices and already sent.
