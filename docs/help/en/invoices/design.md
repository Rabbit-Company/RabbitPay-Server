# Design and wording

Decide how your invoices and emails look and what they say. The language and date format are free for everyone. Your own design, logo and texts are part of the white label license.

## Language, dates and times

The card **Invoice** in **Settings** sets the language of your invoices and of the payment page, and how dates and times are written on them. See [Choose the language of your invoices](getting-started/company-details#choose-the-language-of-your-invoices).

## Change how invoices look

Open **Settings** and find the card **Invoice design**. The preview on the right changes as you choose, and the tabs above it switch between an invoice, a receipt and a credit note. **Open PDF** shows the preview as a real PDF.

![The Invoice design card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/design/invoice-design.webp)

| Setting                             | What it changes                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Layout**                          | **Classic**, **Modern** or **Compact**                                                                                                  |
| **Font**                            | **Sans serif** or **Serif**. Both cover every European language.                                                                        |
| **Accent color**                    | Colors the title and the total. The modern layout also uses it for the top bar and the table headings.                                  |
| **Logo size** and **Logo position** | Where your logo sits and how large it is. The logo itself is uploaded under **License**.                                                |
| **Header text**                     | A short line under the document number                                                                                                  |
| **Footer text**                     | A sentence at the bottom of every document, after the footer from your company details                                                  |
| **Default texts**                   | Texts printed below the items on every invoice, terminal receipt or credit note, for example your payment terms                         |
| **Contact details**                 | Whether your email, phone and website are printed. Your name, address and VAT number are always printed, because the law requires them. |

Press **Save design** when you are done. Each document keeps the design it was issued with, so changing the design never changes an invoice you already sent.

> **Note:** You can try every option in the preview without a license. Saving needs an active white label license. See [What is included](getting-started/licenses).

## Change how emails look

The card **Email design** does the same for the emails your customers receive: the invoice email, the reminder, the receipt and the others. You can change the colors, your logo and the wording of each email. Leave a text empty to keep the standard wording in the language of the project.

![The Email design card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/invoices/design/email-design.webp)

## Use your own logo and sender address

Both are part of white label and are set under **License** in the project menu:

- **Logo** is shown on the payment page, on printed invoices and at the top of emails.
- **Email server** sends invoices, reminders and receipts through your own mail server and from your own address.
