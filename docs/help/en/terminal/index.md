# Terminal

The terminal turns a phone, a tablet, a laptop or a POS device into a till. Use it in a shop, at a market stall, at an event or anywhere you sell in person.

Every sale is an issued invoice with its own receipt, so it shows up in your invoices, payments and reports like any other.

## Before your first sale

- Add what you sell under **Items**, as described in [Items](invoices/items). Each item becomes a tile on the terminal.
- Switch on the [payment methods](payments/payment-methods) your customers may use when they scan the code. Cash needs nothing.
- In Slovenia, set up [fiscal verification](slovenia/fiscal-verification) first. Without it, a Slovenian project cannot take cash at the terminal.
