# Cancel a sale

If the customer changes their mind after you pressed **Charge**, press **Cancel sale**.

The sale already has an invoice number, and numbers cannot be deleted. So RabbitPay asks first and then cancels it with a credit note.

![The question before canceling](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/cancel-sale/confirm.webp)

After canceling you are back in **Current sale**, with the items still there, so you can change them and charge again.

![Back to the sale after canceling](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/cancel-sale/back-to-cart.webp)

## A sale that was already paid

If money was already received, the question says how much. Canceling keeps that payment on record. If you give the money back, record the refund on the invoice, as described in [Return money](invoices/record-payment#return-money).

A cashier cannot cancel a sale that is partly paid. Ask an owner, an admin or a manager.
