# Cashiers and settings

## Give someone the till only

Invite them with the **Cashier** role. Open **Team**, press **Invite teammate**, enter their email and choose **Cashier**.

![Inviting a cashier](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/terminal-settings/cashier.webp)

A cashier signs in straight to the terminal. They can sell and see their own sales of the day, and nothing else in the project: no invoices, customers, reports or settings. See [Invite your team](getting-started/invite-team) for the other roles.

## Custom amounts for cashiers

Open **Settings** and find **Terminal**.

![The Terminal settings](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/terminal-settings/card.webp)

- **On**: cashiers can type any amount on the keypad. Every custom line is recorded with the cashier who entered it.
- **Off**: cashiers can only sell saved items at their saved price. Owners, admins and managers keep the keypad.
