# Receipts

Every sale has a receipt. After the payment, the terminal offers three ways to hand it over.

## Let them scan it

The QR code under the total opens the receipt on the customer's phone, where they can save it. Nothing to print, nothing to type.

## Email it

Type the customer's email address and press **Email receipt**. The terminal shows when it was sent.

![The receipt was emailed](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/receipts/email.webp)

## Print it

Press **Print receipt**. The receipt opens ready to print, with the items, the VAT and the payment.

![A printed receipt](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/receipts/printed.webp)

Press **Print** and choose your printer. **Back to terminal** returns to the till.

## Later

You can get the receipt of any sale of the day again from [Sales today](terminal/sales-today). Older sales are under **Invoices**, like every other invoice.
