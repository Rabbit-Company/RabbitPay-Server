# Make a sale

Add items or a custom amount to the sale, then charge the customer.

## Tap the items

Tap an item to add it to the sale. Tap it again to add one more. The number in the corner of a tile shows how many are in the sale.

![Two posters and three sticker packs in the sale](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/sell/cart.webp)

Prices on the tiles include VAT, the price the customer pays.

## Change the quantity

Use **-** and **+** next to each line in **Current sale**. Pressing **-** on the last one removes the line.

![The quantity buttons of a line](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/sell/quantity.webp)

**Clear** empties the whole sale after asking you.

## Search or scan

Type in **Search or scan a code** to find an item by name or SKU.

![Searching for an item](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/sell/search.webp)

A barcode scanner works too. It types the code and presses Enter, and the item with that SKU is added straight away. Set the barcode of a product as its **SKU** under **Items**.

## Enter a custom amount

For something that is not an item, open **Keypad**.

1. Type the amount. `1500` means 15.00.
2. Optionally write what it is for, for example `Framing service`.
3. Press **Add** with the amount.

![A custom amount of 15.00 for framing](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/sell/keypad.webp)

On a computer you can type the digits on the keyboard and press Enter. If you are registered for VAT, a custom amount includes VAT at the standard rate of your country.

## Charge

When everything is in the sale, press **Charge** with the total.

![The sale is ready to charge](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/sell/ready.webp)

Continue with [Take the payment](terminal/take-payment).
