# Take the payment

After you press **Charge**, the sale becomes an issued invoice and the terminal shows the amount and a QR code.

![The amount to pay and the QR code](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/take-payment/scan.webp)

## The customer pays on their phone

The customer scans the code with their phone camera and pays on the [payment page](payments/payment-page), by card, PayPal or crypto, whatever you switched on. The terminal shows **Waiting for payment** and switches to **Paid** by itself as soon as the payment arrives.

**Copy link** copies the payment link, for example to send it in a message.

## Cash

1. Press **Paid in cash**.
2. Enter the amount the customer handed over in **Cash received**, or press one of the buttons with a round amount or **Exact**.
3. RabbitPay shows the change to give back.
4. Press **Record cash payment**.

   ![Cash received and the change to give back](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/take-payment/cash.webp)

If the customer hands over less than the total, the button records that part in cash and the rest is still to pay, for example by card through the code.

## Paid

The terminal shows **Paid**, the total and, in large letters, the change to give back.

![The sale is paid and shows the change](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/take-payment/paid.webp)

From here you can give the customer a [receipt](terminal/receipts). Press **New sale**, or Enter on a keyboard, for the next customer.

## In Slovenia

With [fiscal verification](slovenia/fiscal-verification), every sale paid in cash, by card or in crypto is sent to FURS the moment it is paid. Terminal sales are numbered with the terminal device, for example `PP1-BLAG1-1`, and the receipt carries the ZOI, the EOR and the QR code for FURS.
