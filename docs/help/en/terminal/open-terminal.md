# Open the terminal

Press **Terminal** in the project menu.

![Terminal in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/open-terminal/nav.webp)

The terminal fills the whole screen. Your items are on the left and the current sale is on the right.

![The terminal, ready for a sale](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/open-terminal/terminal.webp)

## The top bar

![The top bar of the terminal](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/open-terminal/top-bar.webp)

- **Exit** takes you back to the project. A cashier sees **Sign out** instead.
- **Today** lists the sales of the day. See [Sales today](terminal/sales-today).
- The currency box sets the currency of the next sale. Prices of items in another currency are converted at the current rate.
- **Full screen** hides the browser bars, which is handy on a tablet.

While the terminal is open, the screen of your device stays on.

## On a phone

On a narrow screen you see the items first. The button at the bottom shows how many items are in the sale and the total. Tap it to see the sale and charge, and tap **Add more** to go back to the items.

## Pick up where you left off

The current sale is kept on the device. If the browser closes or the battery runs out, open the terminal again and the sale is still there.
