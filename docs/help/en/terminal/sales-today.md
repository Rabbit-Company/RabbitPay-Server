# Sales today

See every sale of the day, with totals to check against the cash drawer.

## Open the list

Press **Today** in the top bar of the terminal.

![Today's sales with the totals](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/terminal/sales-today/dialog.webp)

At the top you see the number of sales, the total, how much came in cash and in other ways, what is still unpaid and how many sales were canceled. That is what you count against the cash drawer at the end of the day.

- Choose **My sales** or **Everyone** to see only your own sales or those of the whole team. Cashiers see only their own.
- **Receipt** opens the receipt of a sale.
- **Open** next to an unpaid sale brings it back to the terminal, so the customer can still pay.
