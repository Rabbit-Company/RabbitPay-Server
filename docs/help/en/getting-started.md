# Getting started

From a new account to your first invoice in a few minutes. Follow the steps in order and you can send an invoice today.

## Create your account

1. Open the home page and press **Get started**.
2. Enter your email address and choose a password. If the server asks for an **Invite code**, enter the one you were given.
3. Accept the terms and press **Create account**.

Your account can protect a lot of money, so switch on two-factor authentication right away. Open **Account security** from the menu under your email address, press **Set up authenticator app** and scan the code with an app such as Aegis or Google Authenticator. Save the recovery codes somewhere safe. They are the only way back in if you lose your phone.

## Create a project

A project is one business. It has its own customers, invoices, numbering, payment methods and team.

1. Press **New project**.
2. Enter the **Project name** and press **Create project**.

If you run more than one business, create a project for each. Their invoices and numbers never mix.

## Fill in your company details

Your invoices are only valid with the right details on them, so do this before you issue anything.

1. Open **Settings** > **Company details** and enter the legal name, address, tax number and registration number.
2. Under **Settings** > **Tax**, choose your **VAT status**. RabbitPay uses it to suggest the right VAT on every invoice.
3. Under **Settings** > **Invoice**, choose the language and the date format of your invoices.

A Slovenian business has a few more things to set up, such as fiscal verification with FURS. See [Slovenian compliance](slovenia).

## Choose how you get paid

Open **Settings** > **Payment methods** and switch on at least one. A bank transfer is the quickest to set up, since it needs only your IBAN. Cards, PayPal and crypto take a few minutes more. See [Payments](payments).

## Issue your first invoice

1. Open **Invoices** and press **New invoice**.
2. Add the customer and the lines.
3. Press **Create and issue**, then send it with **Send** > **Email invoice**.

Everything else about invoices, from pro forma invoices to credit notes, is in [Invoices](invoices). If you sell the same things often, save them once under **Items** and pick them on every invoice.

## Invite your team

Open **Team**, press **Invite teammate**, enter the person's email address, choose a role and press **Send invite**.

| Role          | What they can do                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Owner         | Everything, including deleting the project           |
| Administrator | Everything except deleting the project               |
| Manager       | Payments, invoices, customers and recurring invoices |
| Accountant    | See the financial data and export it                 |
| Cashier       | Sell at the terminal and see their own sales         |
| Viewer        | Look, but not change anything                        |

Give each person the smallest role that lets them do their work.

## What is included and what needs a license

**License** in your project shows how many payments and how much document storage are included, how much you have used, and lets you redeem a license key for more.

The online store and the timesheets, tickets and payroll tools are separate modules that each need their own license. See [Online store](online-store) and [Timesheets, tickets and payroll](workforce).
