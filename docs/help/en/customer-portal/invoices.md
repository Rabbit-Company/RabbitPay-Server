# Invoices in the portal

After signing in, customers see **Your invoices**.

![The invoices of a customer](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/invoices/list.webp)

## Find an invoice

The list shows the reference, the business, the status, the dates, the total and what is still to pay. With **Invoice filter** customers show only **Unpaid**, **Overdue** or **Paid** invoices.

![Only the unpaid invoices](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/invoices/unpaid.webp)

## Open and pay

Pressing a reference opens the invoice the way it was issued, with the payment details and QR codes for a bank transfer.

![An open invoice in the portal](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/invoices/invoice.webp)

- **Pay invoice** opens the [payment page](payments/payment-page) with every method you accept.
- **Download PDF** and **Print** give the customer a copy.
- Credit notes of the invoice are listed under it.
- When the document carries the customer's VAT ID or tax number, they can also download the [e-invoice](slovenia/e-invoices).

## Pro forma invoices

Open [pro forma invoices](invoices/pro-forma) are shown at the top under **Pro forma invoices to pay**, with a PDF and a **Pay** button. The business sends the real invoice once the payment arrives.
