# Orders and profile

## Orders from online stores

Customers who bought in an [online store](online-store) see their orders under **Your orders**, with the store, the date, the payment and the delivery status. **Track package** opens the tracking link once the order is shipped.

![The orders of a customer](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/orders-and-profile/orders.webp)

## Saved details

Under **Profile**, customers save their default billing and delivery details. They are filled in at the next checkout in every store.

![The profile with saved details](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/orders-and-profile/profile.webp)

## Their data

Under **Privacy and your data**:

- **Download my data** gives the customer a file with everything stored about them.
- **Delete my account** removes the profile and the sign in right away.

Issued invoices stay with you after a customer deletes the account, because tax law requires you to keep them.
