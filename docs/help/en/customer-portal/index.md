# Customer portal

The customer portal is where the people and companies you invoice find their documents and pay them. It is separate from the dashboard you work in, and your customers need no password.

| Explain orders, saved details and their data  | [Orders and profile](customer-portal/orders-and-profile) |
| Let customers follow and open support tickets | [Tickets in the portal](customer-portal/tickets)         |

## What you need to do

Almost nothing. Every issued invoice is in the portal for the email address on it.

- Make sure the email on your customer record is the one your customer uses. The email is saved on the invoice when it is issued, so changing a customer's email later does not move earlier invoices to the new address.
- Email must be set up on the server, because customers sign in with a link sent by email.
- Send your customers the link `https://rabbitpay.net/customer/login`, or tell them to choose **Customer portal** on the home page. Your invoice emails also link to the payment page.

Drafts are never shown.
