# Sign in to the portal

Customers sign in with their email address and a link. There is no password to remember.

## Request a link

1. Open `https://rabbitpay.net/customer/login`, or choose **Customer portal** on the home page.
2. Enter the email address your invoices are sent to and press **Send login link**.

   ![Requesting a login link](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/sign-in/request.webp)

3. The portal says the link is on its way.

   ![The link was sent](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/sign-in/sent.webp)

## Open the link

Open the link in the email. It opens a short confirmation, press **Sign in to the customer portal**.

![Confirming the sign in](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/sign-in/confirm.webp)

The link works once and expires after 15 minutes. **Request a new login link** sends another one.

## Several businesses

Customers see the invoices of every business on the same RabbitPay that invoiced their email address, in one list. Each row shows which business it is from.

Shoppers who sign in during checkout in an [online store](online-store) use the same account, with the store's own sign in page.
