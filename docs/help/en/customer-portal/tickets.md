# Tickets in the portal

With [Timesheets, tickets and payroll](workforce), customers can follow their support tickets in the portal, reply and open new ones. They see nothing until you allow it.

## Allow it for a customer

1. Open the customer under **Customers**.
2. In the card **Ticket portal**, switch on **Show tickets in the customer portal**.
3. Choose which kinds of tickets the customer may open, for example support requests.
4. Press **Save changes**.

![Ticket portal access for a customer](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/tickets/access.webp)

The customer then sees the tickets that belong to them and are shared with them, and gets emails when something changes.

## What the customer sees

Under **Tickets** the customer sees their open tickets, who handles them and when they were last updated. **Show** switches between open and resolved tickets.

![The tickets of a customer](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/tickets/list.webp)

A ticket shows the description and the conversation. The customer answers with **Send reply**.

![A ticket with the team's answer and the customer's reply](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/tickets/ticket.webp)

## New tickets

**New ticket** lets the customer choose the type, write a title and a description and press **Send ticket**. It appears in your tickets right away.

![A customer opens a new ticket](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/customer-portal/tickets/new.webp)
