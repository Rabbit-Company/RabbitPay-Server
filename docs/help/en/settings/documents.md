# Language, dates and the issuer

How the invoices you print and the payment page are written, which timezone counts for dates and periods, and who is printed as the issuer.

## The Invoice card

Open **Settings** and find the card **Invoice**.

![The Invoice card](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/settings/documents/invoice.webp)

| Setting                 | What it changes                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Language**            | The language of invoices, receipts, credit notes and the payment page. It is fixed here, so a document reads the same to everyone who opens it.                  |
| **Date format**         | How dates are written, for example 16/10/2026 or 16. 10. 2026                                                                                                    |
| **Time format**         | 14:05, 2:05 PM, or whichever the reader's device uses                                                                                                            |
| **Accounting timezone** | The timezone that decides on which day an invoice is issued, when numbering restarts and which month or year a document belongs to, for example Europe/Ljubljana |

The sentence under the fields shows how a date and a time look with your choice. Press **Save invoice settings**.

Choose the accounting timezone of the country where you keep your books, and leave it unchanged once you have issued invoices. Changing it later can move documents near midnight into another day.

## Print the issuer and signature

Some customers and public bodies want to see who issued an invoice. Switch on **Show invoice issuer and signature** and save. Invoices then print the company's legal name, the full name of the member who issued the invoice and their drawn signature at the bottom right.

Each member sets their own name and signature:

1. Open **Team** in the project menu.
2. Press **Invoice identity** on your own row.
3. Check your **Full name**.
4. Draw your **Signature** with a mouse, pen or finger. **Clear signature** starts again.
5. Press **Save changes**.

![A name and drawn signature](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/settings/documents/identity.webp)

This is a picture of a signature, not a qualified electronic signature. Invoices that already exist keep the issuer they were issued with.
