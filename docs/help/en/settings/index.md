# Settings

Every setting of a project and the page that explains it, card by card, plus the settings of your own account.

## Project settings

Press **Settings** in the project menu. The page is a column of cards. Only owners and administrators can change most of them.

![Settings in the project menu](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/settings/index/nav.webp)

| Card                           | What it sets                                                              | Page                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Details**                    | The project and display name, and the primary currency                    | [Change the name or the currency](getting-started/create-project#change-the-name-or-the-currency) |
| **Company details**            | Legal name, address, tax and registration numbers, invoice footer         | [Enter your company details](getting-started/company-details#enter-your-company-details)          |
| **Invoice**                    | Language, date and time format, accounting timezone, issuer and signature | [Language, dates and the issuer](settings/documents)                                              |
| **Invoice design**             | Layout, font, colors and texts of invoices                                | [Design and wording](invoices/design)                                                             |
| **Email design**               | Colors, logo and wording of emails                                        | [Design and wording](invoices/design#change-how-emails-look)                                      |
| **Document numbers**           | How invoices, pro forma invoices and orders are numbered                  | [Numbering](invoices/numbering)                                                                   |
| **Tax**                        | Country, VAT status, reporting currency, OSS                              | [Set how VAT applies to you](getting-started/company-details#set-how-vat-applies-to-you)          |
| **Fiscal verification (FURS)** | Certificate, business premises and devices, Slovenia only                 | [Fiscal verification](slovenia/fiscal-verification)                                               |
| **e-Invoice signing**          | The certificate that signs e-SLOG invoices                                | [Sign your e-invoices](slovenia/e-invoices#sign-your-e-invoices)                                  |
| **Accent color**               | The color of buttons on the payment page and in this project              | [Brand color](settings/brand-color)                                                               |
| **Email**                      | What emails carry and automatic reminders                                 | [Send reminders automatically](invoices/send#send-reminders-automatically)                        |
| **Terminal**                   | Whether cashiers can type their own amounts                               | [Terminal settings](terminal/terminal-settings)                                                   |
| **Payment methods**            | Bank transfer, cards, PayPal and crypto                                   | [Payment methods](payments/payment-methods)                                                       |
| **API keys** and **Webhooks**  | Connecting other software                                                 | [API keys and webhooks](settings/api-keys-and-webhooks)                                           |
| **Delete project**             | Removing the project                                                      | [Delete a project](settings/delete-project)                                                       |

## Your account

Press your email address in the top right corner and choose **Account security**. The **Security** tab has your password and the second sign-in step. The **Notifications** tab chooses what RabbitPay tells you about.

See [Protect your account](getting-started/secure-your-account) and [Choose your notifications](getting-started/notifications).
