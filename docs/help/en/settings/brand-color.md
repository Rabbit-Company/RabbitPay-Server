# Brand color

The color of buttons and highlights on the payment page your customers see, and in RabbitPay while you work in this project.

## Choose a color

Open **Settings** and find the card **Accent color**.

1. Press one of the colors, or the last circle to pick any color you like.
2. The preview below shows a pay button, a link and a status in that color.
3. Press **Save color**.

![The Accent color card with green chosen](https://cdn.rabbitpay.net/help/en/dark/settings/brand-color/card.webp)

**Use default** goes back to the RabbitPay blue.

## Colors on invoices and emails

The accent color here is for the payment page and the dashboard. Invoices and emails have their own colors, set in **Invoice design** and **Email design**. See [Design and wording](invoices/design).
